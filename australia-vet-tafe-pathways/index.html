Australia VET / TAFE Pathway Map

OTC is building a structured vocational pathway screening layer for Australian TAFE, VET, English preparation, diploma, advanced diploma and TAFE-to-university progression routes.

Australia route AI operations
Operating Purpose

Vocational education gives OTC a practical Australia-facing service route.

VET and TAFE routes sit between school, English preparation, university progression and career qualification planning. OTC's first step is to maintain a state-by-state screening map so that students can be guided by course level, occupation direction, English readiness, document requirements and institution process before any formal application or professional referral is made.

Screening Matrix

What OTC should check before recommending a VET / TAFE route.

Course levelCertificate, Diploma, Advanced Diploma

Confirm level, duration, delivery mode, placement requirements and progression options.

English readinessEntry score and preparation route

Check English requirements, accepted tests, ELICOS options and realistic timing.

Occupation linkCareer direction without overclaiming

Record the student's target field while keeping skills assessment and migration advice separate.

University pathwayCredit and progression possibility

Identify TAFE-to-university routes, advanced standing possibilities and institution-specific limits.

DocumentsReadiness and file discipline

Prepare academic records, passport, English evidence, CV, employment evidence and translations where needed.

Professional boundaryReferral when advice is regulated

Route migration, legal, tax, skills assessment and professional registration questions to qualified advisers.

Next Build Step

Convert this map into provider approach records.

For each priority provider, OTC should create a private record with provider website, international contact, agent / representative application route, required company documents, training requirements, approved course areas, published policies and the date of last review. The public website should remain an index, while contracts and commercial channels stay private.
