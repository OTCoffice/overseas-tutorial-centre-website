Profile

一所位於 Tweed Coast / Northern NSW 的 K-12 聖公會學校。

Lindisfarne Anglican Grammar School is a co-educational Preschool to Year 12 Anglican school serving the southern Gold Coast, Tweed Coast and northern New South Wales region. The school operates two campuses and supports international students through structured enrolment, wellbeing and homestay arrangements.

LocationTweed Coast, NSW約 15 分鐘至 Gold Coast International Airport，約 90 分鐘至 Brisbane International Airport。
CampusesTwo campusesJunior School: Sunshine Avenue Campus. Middle and Senior School: Mahers Lane Campus, Terranora.
Age rangePreschool to Year 12One school, two campuses, three subschools: Junior, Middle and Senior School.

China Family Lens對中國家庭的真正吸引力：靠近 Gold Coast，但不是純旅遊城市學校。

Lindisfarne 的位置對中國家庭很容易理解：15 分鐘到 Gold Coast International Airport，90 分鐘到 Brisbane International Airport，Tweed Coast 兼有海岸生活、澳洲本地家庭社群和相對安靜的學習環境。對低齡學生來說，這種「有機場、有城市、有海岸、但校園生活仍集中」的組合，比單純大城市學校更容易形成穩定作息。

英語沉浸

學生不是只在語言班學英文，而是在 classroom、homestay、sports、music、service activities 裡持續使用英文。

升學準備

高年級有 careers class、work experience、university sessions、campus tours、scholarship workshops 和個別 pathways counselling。

照護結構

Wellbeing team、health facilities、campus safety、homestay monitoring 對第一次離家的學生尤其重要。

短期試讀

Short-term schedule 適合先體驗澳洲校園、homestay 和英文課堂，再決定是否轉長期入讀。

Academic And Student Support

學術、升學與國際學生支持。

Prospectus materials describe a K-12 learning journey, university guidance in senior years, wellbeing support and international student accommodation arrangements.

01K-12 連續路線

Junior School 覆蓋 Preschool / Kindergarten 至 Year 4；Middle School 為 Years 5-8；Senior School 為 Years 9-12，銜接 NSW curriculum 與高年級升學準備。

02University guidance

高年級學生可接觸 careers classes、work experience、university sessions、campus tours、scholarship and entry workshops 等升學與職業探索活動。

03Wellbeing team

學校材料列明 nurses、psychologists、counsellors、chaplains 及 Learning Enrichment Department 支持學生適應、身心健康與學業發展。

04Homestay and welfare

Year 7-12 國際學生如未與家庭同住，可通過學校 homestay programme 安排住宿和照護；homestay families 由相關 provider 協助篩選與監察。

Decision Notes

中國家庭選 Lindisfarne 時，OTC 會先看這幾件事。

年級窗口

長期路線可看 K-Year 12；Year 7-12 若涉及 homestay/welfare，要同步評估成熟度和英文適應。

英文要求

Year 7-9 可參考 IELTS 5.0 / AEAS 46-56；Year 10-11 要看更高英文門檻及面試可能性。

家庭預算

除 tuition/levies 外，還要把 OSHC、uniform、homestay、airport transfer、excursions、bus route 等放入總預算。

短期或長期

如果家庭未確定是否長期澳洲路線，可先用 short-term / one-term experience 測試學生適應度。

Application Route

申請流程與 OTC 初篩。

The prospectus lists an international enrolment pathway from application form to assessment, interview, offer, written agreement, CoE/CAAW, visa process, homestay application, English checkpoint and orientation.

  1. Family intakeOTC 收集學生年齡、當前年級、成績單、英文水平、目標入學時間及住宿偏好。
  2. Document screening對照學校年級、英文要求、住宿與費用安排，形成中文家庭說明。
  3. School confirmation向學校確認 application form、agent process、offer pathway 及正式文件清單。
  4. Formal application以學校正式要求為準提交文件，等待 assessment / interview / outcome。
Professional boundary

OTC can support education-route explanation, family communication and document organisation. Visa, legal, migration, financial and regulated advice should be handled by qualified professionals where required.
