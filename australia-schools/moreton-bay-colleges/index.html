Brisbane Route

一組 Brisbane 東區的男校/女校學校組合。

The Moreton Bay Colleges comprise Moreton Bay College and Moreton Bay Boys' College. Prospectus materials describe two campuses with premium facilities, a natural environment, international preparation courses, Primary Years Programme, junior/senior secondary studies and school-managed accommodation.

China Family LensMoreton Bay 的優勢，是「Brisbane 城市資源 + 男校/女校分流 + 過渡課程」。

對中國家庭來說，Moreton Bay Colleges 比較容易放入 Brisbane 升學版圖：城市安全感、距 CBD 較近、可選女校或男校、PSP/HSP 幫學生先適應澳洲學校，再進入 mainstream learning。對英文仍在過渡期、但家庭希望保留 Brisbane 大學與城市資源的學生，這條路線非常清晰。

準備課程

20-week PSP/HSP 用於銜接 primary 或 high school，強調英文、study skills、critical thinking 和部分 mainstream integration。

小學 PYP

Primary School Prep-Year 6 採 IB PYP 理念，重視 inquiry、whole-child development 和 international-mindedness。

高年級出口

Years 11-12 可銜接 QCE、ATAR 或 VET qualification，對未來大學與職業方向都保留彈性。

住宿支持

Prospectus 描述 school-managed homestay、24-hour support、meals、transport and security checking。

Programme Map

從準備課程到 QCE / ATAR / VET 的 Brisbane 路線。

01PSP / HSP

Prospectus lists 20-week Primary School Preparation and High School Preparation courses for international students transitioning into Australian school.

02Primary PYP

Primary School Prep-Year 6 follows International Baccalaureate Primary Years Programme principles, focusing on whole-child development.

03Secondary

Years 7-10 follow Australian Curriculum; Years 11-12 can lead to QCE, ATAR and/or VET qualification pathways.

04Short-term routes

Prospectus includes study abroad, study tours, buddy programme and group short-term experiences, with January/April/July/October entry windows for individual study abroad.

Decision Notes

Moreton Bay 適合希望「先過渡、再主流」的學生。

男校/女校選擇

家庭需要先判斷學生更適合 girls' college、boys' college 或 siblings 分校就讀安排。

PSP/HSP 判斷

英文、學術習慣和面試表現會影響 direct entry 或 preparation course route。

Brisbane 城市資源

距 CBD 近、鄰近 Moreton Bay，並可連接 UQ、QUT、Griffith、ACU、CQU 等城市高教資源。

短期體驗

Study abroad、study tours、buddy programme 適合先看校園和英文適應，再決定長期入讀。

Application Route

Moreton Bay Colleges 申請路線摘要。

Prospectus lists a five-step route: apply with passport, academic records and English evidence; eligibility/interview; offer and written agreement; visa with CoE/CAAW; pre-arrival including homestay, airport transfer and uniform fitting.

  1. Student intake確認男校/女校、年級、英文程度、PSP/HSP 是否需要。
  2. Documents護照、近兩年成績單、英文能力證明、subject selection 如適用。
  3. Interview route由學校安排 Principal interview，確認 academic/co-curricular interests。
  4. Offer and CoE以學校 Provisional Letter of Offer、Written Agreement、CoE/CAAW 為準。
