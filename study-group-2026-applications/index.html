OTC Application Workflow

What OTC checks before recommending a route.

  1. Student profile: current school or university, qualification stage, target subject and preferred intake.
  2. Academic evidence: transcript, grading scale, course descriptions, diploma or enrolment confirmation where relevant.
  3. English readiness: IELTS, TOEFL, PTE, Duolingo or internal English plan where accepted.
  4. Route match: foundation, International Year One, pre-master, direct entry or alternative destination.
  5. Deadline control: missing-document list, application timeline and confirmation follow-up.
Send OTC a route review request

Include current qualification, latest transcript, English score, target subject, preferred destination and whether the student can make a quick confirmation decision.
