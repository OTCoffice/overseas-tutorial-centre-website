About the Organisation

Overseas Tutorial Centre

A multi-function education, tutorial and publishing ecosystem serving learners, families and overseas Chinese communities.

Business Portfolio

OTC operates as an education, services, publishing and digital-learning portfolio.

Overseas Tutorial Centre connects advisory work, institutional services, course support, publishing products and learning technology under one operating structure. Each business area has its own public entrance, so students, families, institutions and professional partners can move directly to the service line that matches their need.
