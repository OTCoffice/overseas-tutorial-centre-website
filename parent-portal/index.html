Reusable Proposal Template

規劃書模板骨架

Each future family proposal can follow this same structure: student facts, route diagnosis, English condition, institution shortlist, OTC service scope and next-document checklist.

01 Student snapshot

Current study year, date of birth, family location, preferred living arrangement, target intake and academic score pattern.

02 Route diagnosis

Identify whether the student needs direct entry, foundation, OTHM Level 3, BTEC, International Year One or another progression route.

03 English gatekeeper

Record the English evidence required for admissions and separate school-entry readiness from visa and CAS conditions.

04 University shortlist

Rank realistic institutions by intake, location, subject fit, progression recognition, commute feasibility and documentation burden.

05 Service scope

Define what OTC handles, what the family provides, third-party fees, university decisions and non-guarantee boundaries.

06 Next documents

Turn the plan into a clean checklist: transcript, passport, English evidence, subject preference, family consent and living notes.

Next documents

High-school transcript screenshots, English evidence, passport details, Stratford living-arrangement note and subject preference.

English gatekeeper

English score and academic-writing readiness remain the key condition before confirming university application strategy.

Admissions note

University entry, intake availability, English condition and CAS eligibility must be confirmed with each receiving institution.

This first portal layer is a lightweight client-access interface for proposal viewing and case coordination. For long-term use, sensitive passports, transcripts and financial evidence should move through production authentication and agreed secure channels.
