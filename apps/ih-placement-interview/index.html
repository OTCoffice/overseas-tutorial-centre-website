What the app prepares for

IH London public guidance describes an online level placement test before the course and a one-to-one teacher interview/conversation to further assess and clarify English level. IH Young Learner guidance also describes initial online assessment and placement into level- and age-appropriate classes. OTC therefore prepares readiness, speaking confidence and vocabulary, not official answers.

Online level testBefore course start

Practice: grammar control, everyday vocabulary, short reading logic and common learner-error review.

Speaking interviewWeek before course / teacher check

Practice: clear self-introduction, school and hobbies answers, London expectations and polite repair phrases.

Young learner placementAge and level fit

Practice: natural, honest answers that help the school place the learner appropriately.

Zoom readinessOnline communication

Practice: camera, microphone, full-name identification, polite question repair and safe online behaviour.
