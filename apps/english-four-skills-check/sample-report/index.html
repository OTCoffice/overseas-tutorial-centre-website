三、寫作證據與改善建議

Task 1

You cannot attend your study group this Friday. Write an email to the organiser. Explain why, ask what you will miss, and suggest how you can catch up. You may invent the details.

分析原文
Dear Alex,
I am sorry that I cannot attend our study group this Friday because I have a appointment at the same time. Could you tell me which chapters the group will discuss? I can read them on Saturday and send my notes to you. If there is any group work, please let me know how I can help from home. Thank you for your understanding.
Best wishes,
Sam
面向原文證據分析下一步
任務回應Could you tell me which chapters the group will discuss?明確詢問缺席時會討論哪些章節，對應題目要求。補上預計何時寄出補讀筆記，讓方案更可執行。
語法a appointmentappointment 以母音音素起首，此處用 an appointment。以 an appointment、an example、a meeting 各造一句。

Task 2

Some students prefer to study alone; others prefer to study with a group. Which approach works better for you? Explain your view with reasons and a specific example. You may also mention a limitation of your preferred approach.

分析原文
I prefer studying with a small group because I can ask questions when I do not understand an idea. Other students sometimes explain the same topic in a different way, which helps me remember it. Last month, my friends and I prepared a presentation about public transport. I understood the reading, but I was not sure how to organise my part. One friend suggested three main points, and I practised explaining them to the group. Their questions helped me notice where I needed more detail. However, group study is not always efficient. We sometimes talk about unrelated things and lose time. For this reason, I think everyone should prepare alone first and agree on a clear task before meeting. This make our group more useful and gives quieter students time to think.
面向原文證據分析下一步
具體例子One friend suggested three main points指出同伴提供的實際協助，但三個重點內容與修改成果仍可具體化。加一句說明其中一個重點及它如何改善你的段落。
權衡We sometimes talk about unrelated things and lose time.承認小組學習的限制，後文用先準備與約定任務作回應。寫出一項可執行的小組時間規則。
主謂一致This make our group more usefulThis 是單數主詞，現在式動詞應用 makes。找出段落每個主詞與動詞，逐一檢查一致性。
