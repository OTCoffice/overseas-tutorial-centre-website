OTC English Check｜英文四項初步診斷

品牌主標語｜Main message

先了解英文起點，再決定怎樣學。

Understand your starting point. Plan your next step.

免費的聽力、閱讀、口說與寫作初步診斷工具，由海外督導 Overseas Tutorial Centre（OTC）製作。適合尚未考過正式英文檢定，或希望在開始學習及備考前，先整理四項能力表現的學習者。

A free initial English diagnostic from Overseas Tutorial Centre (OTC), covering listening, reading, speaking and writing. Designed for learners who have not taken a formal English test, or who want a clearer starting point before planning their learning or exam preparation.

1. App 詳細介紹｜About the app

「我的英文大概在哪裡？應該先補基礎，還是開始準備考試？」如果你也有這些問題，可以先從一份真實作答開始。

OTC English Check 透過四項短任務，整理你在理解英文資訊、表達想法及組織內容方面的初步表現。聽力與閱讀依答案自動計分；口說錄音與寫作原稿則提供給老師進一步評閱，協助後續討論更有依據。

沒有正式考試成績也能使用。若手上只有學校或補習班內部測驗，可以保留作為背景資料，再用這份工具補充四項作答樣本；不會把不同測驗的成績直接換算成 IELTS 分數。

“Where should I start with English? Should I build my foundations or begin exam preparation?” A sample of your own work is a useful first step.

OTC English Check uses four short tasks to capture how you understand information, express ideas and organise a response. Listening and reading are checked against an answer key. Your speaking recordings and original writing can then support a more informed discussion with a teacher.

You do not need an official test result to use the tool. School or language-centre results may provide background, while these tasks add fresh samples of your work. Results from different tests are not converted into IELTS scores.

2. 適合誰使用？｜Who is it for?

  • 尚未考過正式英文檢定，希望先了解學習起點的學生。
  • 考慮 IELTS、TOEFL 或海外升學，但還不確定應優先練習哪些能力的學習者。
  • 希望在初次諮詢或試教前，準備具體作答資料的學生與家長。
  • 希望用同一套短任務收集新生作答，再進行人工評閱的老師。
  • Students who have not taken a formal English test and want a starting point.
  • Learners considering IELTS, TOEFL or overseas study who are unsure which skills to prioritise.
  • Students and families who want concrete work samples for an initial consultation or trial lesson.
  • Teachers who want a consistent set of introductory tasks to support their own review.

這是一般英語初步工具，並非幼兒測評、專業英語認證或特定考試模擬卷。若題目明顯太容易或太困難，請在報告備註，方便老師另選合適材料。

This is an initial general-English tool, not an assessment for very young children, a specialist qualification or a replica of a particular exam. If the tasks feel much too easy or difficult, note this in your report so a teacher can choose more suitable material.

3. 四項內容｜What is included?

聽力 Listening｜約 8 分鐘 / About 8 minutes

三段原創英語音檔，共 12 題選擇題。內容包括學習安排、活動變更及對一項試行計畫的分析，從具體資訊延伸至原因、條件及說話者觀點。建議先聽一遍，必要時重播；播放啟動次數會記入報告，讓老師了解作答條件。音檔使用合成英語語音。

Three original English recordings with 12 multiple-choice questions. Topics include study arrangements, a change to an activity and a review of a trial programme. Questions cover details, reasons, conditions and the speaker’s position. Listen once first and replay if needed. The report records play starts to help explain the conditions of your attempt. Recordings use synthetic English speech.

閱讀 Reading｜約 8 分鐘 / About 8 minutes

三篇原創短文，共 12 題選擇題，涵蓋通知、學習經驗及觀點分析。除了找出資訊，也需要分辨文本支持甚麼結論、哪些推論尚未有足夠證據。

Three original passages with 12 multiple-choice questions, covering a notice, a learning experience and an analytical discussion. Tasks include locating information, understanding implications and distinguishing supported conclusions from claims that need more evidence.

口說 Speaking｜約 6 分鐘 / About 6 minutes

兩個口說任務：描述熟悉的地方，以及分享一次學習經驗。每題先準備約 30 秒，再以英文回答，建議錄製 90 秒及 120 秒。可在瀏覽器錄音，或選取已有音檔。錄音可重錄、回聽及下載；錄音本身不會產生自動程度分數。

Two speaking tasks: describe a familiar place and discuss an experience of learning something difficult. Prepare for about 30 seconds per task, then answer in English for approximately 90 and 120 seconds respectively. Record in the browser or select an existing audio file. You can listen back, replace and download recordings. The recording feature does not automatically grade your speaking.

寫作 Writing｜約 15 分鐘 / About 15 minutes

兩項寫作任務：60–90 字的短電郵，以及 120–180 字的意見段落。提供字數提示及可選計時器，保留原始作答供老師評閱。時間與字數是練習指引，並非自動評分依據。

Two writing tasks: a short email of 60–90 words and an opinion paragraph of 120–180 words. Word counts and optional timers help you manage the tasks. Your original responses are retained for teacher review. Time and word-count guidance are not automatic grading criteria.

4. 逐步使用方法｜Step-by-step guide

第一步：準備好環境｜1. Get ready

預留約 35–45 分鐘，也可以分段完成。找一個安靜的位置，準備耳機及可用的麥克風。用電腦輸入寫作通常較方便。開始錄音時，瀏覽器會要求麥克風權限。

Allow about 35–45 minutes, or complete the tasks in stages. Choose a quiet place and have headphones and a working microphone ready. A computer is usually more convenient for writing. Your browser will request microphone access when you start recording.

第二步：填寫學習代號｜2. Choose an optional alias

可使用 Learner-01 等代號，不必輸入真名。這份診斷不需要護照、證件號碼或住址。

Use an alias such as Learner-01 if you wish. Your real name, passport details, identification numbers and home address are not needed for these tasks.

第三步：獨立完成聽力與閱讀｜3. Complete listening and reading independently

先按自己的理解作答，不使用翻譯、字典、AI 或他人代答。不會的可以留空；不要為了湊齊答案而隨意猜選。若有重播、查字或其他協助，請在報告備註，保留真實情況。

Answer from your own understanding, without translation, a dictionary, AI or someone answering for you. Leave uncertain items blank instead of guessing just to complete every question. Note any replays, lookups or other help in your report.

第四步：錄製口說｜4. Record your speaking

讀完題目後先簡單想好內容，再自然說明。用自己的經驗及例子，不需要背範文。錄製後回聽，確認聲音清楚。若麥克風無法使用，可用手機另行錄製，再選取音檔。每個音檔上限為 25 MB；瀏覽器每次錄音最多三分鐘。

Read the prompt, briefly plan your response and speak naturally. Use your own experiences and examples; do not memorise a model answer. Listen back to check that your voice is audible. If browser recording is unavailable, record on your phone and select the file instead. Selected files must be no larger than 25 MB; browser recordings are limited to three minutes each.

第五步：完成寫作｜5. Complete the writing tasks

直接用英文寫，盡量在建議時間內完成。保留你原本會使用的字詞與句型，不需要先請別人潤色。若寫不完，也可以保留目前內容並說明原因。

Write directly in English and aim to work within the suggested time. Use your own vocabulary and sentence structures without having someone polish the response first. If you cannot finish, keep what you have written and explain why.

第六步：查看及保存報告｜6. Review and save your report

前往「我的報告」查看聽讀答對數、已答／未答題數，以及口說與寫作的完成情況。完成第一次作答後，再展開聽讀答案與解釋。

下載 TXT 文字報告；如需要保留結構化資料，可另下載 JSON。也可用列印功能另存 PDF。兩份口說音檔必須另外下載，音檔不會嵌入文字或 JSON 報告。

Open “My report” to see listening and reading results, answered and unanswered counts, and the completion status of speaking and writing. Expand the answer explanations after your first attempt.

Download the TXT report. A JSON download is also available for structured data, and your print dialog may offer Save as PDF. Download each speaking recording separately: audio files are not embedded in the TXT or JSON report.

第七步：按需要安排老師評閱｜7. Arrange teacher review if needed

如希望進一步了解學習重點，可聯絡 office@overseasuk.com，先確認評閱安排及費用，再提供報告和音檔。工具不會自動傳送資料；點擊電郵連結後，仍需自行附上檔案。

To discuss learning priorities, contact office@overseasuk.com and confirm review arrangements and any fees before sharing your report and audio. The tool does not automatically send your work. Email links open without attachments, so you must attach the files yourself.

5. 怎樣理解結果？｜Understanding the results

聽力與閱讀：每項最多答對 12 題，另列出作答數量。這些結果只反映本次短任務的表現。部分作答、重播次數及使用協助的情況都會影響解讀；未作答不等於程度低。

Listening and reading: Each section has 12 questions. The report shows correct answers and completion counts. Results describe performance on these short tasks only. Partial completion, replays and assistance affect interpretation; unanswered questions do not establish low proficiency.

口說與寫作：顯示作答或音檔是否備妥，並標示「待老師評閱」。完成任務、錄製時間或字數，都不能單獨代表語言程度。

Speaking and writing: The report shows whether responses or recordings are available and labels them for teacher review. Completion, recording length and word count do not establish language proficiency on their own.

這份工具不提供：IELTS／TOEFL 成績換算、正式 CEFR 等級、四項綜合程度認證、錄取或獎學金判定，以及保證提分的結論。重做同一組題目的改善可能包含記憶效果，不宜視為正式進步測量。

The tool does not provide: IELTS or TOEFL score conversions, certified CEFR levels, an overall four-skills qualification, admissions or scholarship decisions, or guaranteed score improvements. Better results on repeated questions may reflect familiarity and should not be treated as a formal measure of progress.

6. 老師評閱建議｜Teacher review guidance

口說可從內容是否切題、表達是否連貫、字詞及句型運用、發音是否容易理解四個面向記錄觀察。寫作可從任務完成度、組織與連接、詞彙運用、語法與句子清晰度整理回饋。

每項建議記下：一個具體優點、一段需要改善的原始例子，以及一個可執行的練習。之後可安排新題目或二次作答，觀察改善是否能用於不同情境。這是教學回饋框架，不是官方考試評分規準。

For speaking, review relevance, coherence, vocabulary and sentence use, and how easily the pronunciation can be understood. For writing, review task completion, organisation and linking, vocabulary, and grammar and sentence clarity.

For each area, record a specific strength, an example from the learner’s work that needs attention, and one practical exercise. A new prompt or follow-up response can help show whether the learner can apply the feedback. This is a teaching framework, not an official examination rubric.

7. 免費範圍與資料保存｜Free access and saving your work

Payhip 定價：免費。免費範圍是本初步診斷工具及使用指南。人工評閱、個別學習規劃、課程或考試報名，均不包含在免費下載內；如需要相關服務，須另行確認安排及費用。免費下載不代表已預約老師。

Payhip price: Free. Free access covers the initial diagnostic tool and its guide. Personal marking, individual learning plans, lessons and exam bookings are not included. Any additional services require a separate agreement on availability and fees. Downloading the free resource does not book a teacher.

文字與選項儲存在目前瀏覽器，可在同一瀏覽器繼續；清除瀏覽器資料或使用無痕模式可能影響保存。錄音只保留在目前頁面，重新整理或關閉頁面前請下載。共用裝置完成後，先下載所需資料，再按「清除本機紀錄」。

Text and selections are saved in the current browser so you can continue there. Private browsing or clearing browser data may affect retention. Audio lasts only for the current page session, so download it before refreshing or closing the page. On shared devices, download what you need and then clear the saved work.

App 不會自動把作答或錄音上傳給 OTC。使用者自行決定是否分享。Payhip 的下載流程及網站託管服務可能另有其帳戶、連線紀錄及私隱安排，請依各平台當時顯示的條款使用。

The app does not automatically upload answers or recordings to OTC. You choose whether to share them. Payhip’s download process and the website’s hosting service have their own account, connection-log and privacy arrangements; refer to the terms shown by each platform.

8. 常見問題｜Frequently asked questions

沒考過 IELTS，也可以用嗎？｜Can I use it without an IELTS result?

可以。這正適合先建立作答樣本、再討論下一步的學習者。

Yes. It is designed to help you gather work samples before discussing your next steps.

完成後會知道 IELTS 幾分嗎？｜Will it tell me my IELTS band?

不會。短診斷不能直接換算正式考試成績。

No. This short diagnostic cannot be converted into an official exam score.

可以分開幾次做嗎？｜Can I complete it in stages?

可以。文字及選项可保存在同一瀏覽器；口說音檔請在離開頁面前下載。

Yes. Text and selections can be retained in the same browser. Download speaking files before leaving the page.

免費是否包括老師批改？｜Does free access include teacher marking?

不包括。老師評閱需另外聯絡 OTC，確認可用安排及費用。

No. Contact OTC separately to confirm review availability and fees.

沒有麥克風或不能錄音怎麼辦？｜What if recording does not work?

可選取以手機錄製的音檔；也可先完成其他項目，並在報告註明口說尚未完成。

Select an audio file recorded on your phone, or complete the other sections and note that speaking is outstanding.
