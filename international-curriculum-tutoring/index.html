International Curriculum Tutoring

A-Level, BTEC, IB and AP bilingual tutoring

雙語輔導 for Chinese EAL learners in international schools, international departments and pathway programmes who need to move from subject understanding to confident English academic output.

Course support is grouped by curriculum type and learning pain point.

This service line sits between Courses, Academic Tutoring and Overseas Publishing. Tutoring helps students understand concepts, plan coursework, read rubrics, build academic vocabulary and practise responsible written output. It is not official school delivery, official examination training, model-answer sale or assignment completion.

Best-fit learners

  1. Chinese international-school students moving from bilingual learning into English-medium assessment.
  2. BTEC, IB and coursework-heavy learners who need planning and writing structure.
  3. A-Level, IGCSE and AP students who understand concepts but underperform in English output.

Academic boundary

OTC can explain, coach, review requirements and discuss structure. OTC cannot write assessed work, provide ready-made submission answers, guarantee grades or present itself as an official awarding-body service.
