This service line sits between Courses, Academic Tutoring and Overseas Publishing. It is not official school delivery, official examination training, model-answer sale or assignment completion.

BTEC / Pearson Vocational Focus

BTEC courses that can support undergraduate progression planning.

BTEC is especially suitable for Chinese learners who are stronger in practical projects than written examinations, but who still need English academic writing, evidence planning and portfolio discipline. OTC's tutoring focuses on programme-fit, assignment understanding and university-ready academic output.

01 Business 本科方向：Business Management, Marketing, Accounting, International Business, Entrepreneurship

Suitable for students aiming at business-related undergraduate degrees and needing stronger report writing, case analysis, market research and evaluation paragraphs.

OTC support: unit concept tutoring, business case evidence, P/M/D criteria reading, report structure and academic phrase banks.
02 Information Technology 本科方向：Computing, IT, Cyber Security, Data, Digital Business

Suitable for learners moving from practical IT units into university courses where technical explanation, project documentation and reflective evaluation matter.

OTC support: project brief breakdown, technical vocabulary, evidence logs, user-requirement writing and evaluation of digital solutions.
03 Creative Media 本科方向：Media Production, Digital Media, Film, Communication, Creative Industries

Suitable for portfolio-based applicants who must explain research, planning, production decisions and creative outcomes in credible English.

OTC support: research journal structure, production rationale, portfolio captions, reflective writing and presentation scripts.
04 Hospitality 本科方向：Hospitality Management, Tourism, Events, Hotel Management, Service Business

Suitable for students aiming at management-focused hospitality or tourism degrees and needing business-style analysis of operations, service quality and customer experience.

OTC support: operations case analysis, service-quality evidence, customer journey explanation, business report writing and presentation coaching.
05 Health & Social Care 本科方向：Health & Social Care, Public Health, Social Policy, Care Management

Suitable for students applying to non-clinical health, care, public-health or social-care related degrees. Clinical or regulated professional routes may have extra entry rules.

OTC support: safeguarding vocabulary, care-value explanation, policy evidence, reflective writing and ethical discussion structure.
Route diagnosis 升學路線診斷

Check whether the learner is using BTEC International Level 3, BTEC National, HNC, HND or another Pearson route, then map units to intended undergraduate majors.

Assignment brief breakdown 任務書拆解

Turn each assignment brief into task verbs, evidence requirements, source needs, assessment criteria and a weekly action plan.

Pass / Merit / Distinction awareness P/M/D 標準理解

Help students understand the difference between describing, explaining, analysing and evaluating, without providing ready-made submission answers.

University application link 本科申請銜接

Organise unit strengths, project evidence, portfolio highlights and subject vocabulary for personal statement, interview and progression discussions.

Progression note: BTEC qualifications may support undergraduate entry or advanced-entry discussion depending on the exact qualification size, grade profile, subject match, English requirement and the receiving university's rules. OTC provides tutoring and progression planning only; it does not guarantee admission, credit transfer, grades or university acceptance.

Essay and exam-response coaching 英文答題與論文段落輔導

Course-linked academic vocabulary, phrase banks, evaluation verbs and bilingual study companion resources from Overseas Publishing.

Best-fit learners

  1. Chinese international-school students moving from bilingual learning into English-medium assessment.
  2. BTEC, IB and coursework-heavy learners who need planning and writing structure.
  3. A-Level, IGCSE and AP students who understand concepts but underperform in English output.

Academic boundary

OTC can explain, coach, review requirements and discuss structure. OTC cannot write assessed work, provide ready-made submission answers, guarantee grades or present itself as an official awarding-body service.
