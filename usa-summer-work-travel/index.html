Know the roles

OTC supports the application; the designated Sponsor performs the regulated programme functions.

Only a U.S. Department of State-designated Sponsor may issue Form DS-2019. OTC does not present itself as a Sponsor or replace decisions made by consular officers, employers or housing providers.

Application route

Six stages from eligibility screening to arrival support.

At each stage, confirm the responsible party, required documents, fees and unresolved risks before moving forward.

01Free eligibility screen

Check full-time post-secondary status, age, English, vacation dates and the required return-to-study date.

02Sponsor route comparison

Compare programme models, fees, job arrangements and application windows of designated Sponsors.

03Application preparation

Organise documents, résumé, English interview practice, employer information and the timeline.

04Job and housing checks

Review duties, hours, pay, location, housing, transport and living costs without guaranteeing outcomes.

05Visa file organisation

Organise post-DS-2019 and visa materials against Sponsor and official instructions; no immigration legal advice.

06Pre-departure support

Prepare insurance, emergency contacts, arrival, check-in, parent updates and escalation steps.

Service boundary

Overseas Tutorial Centre is not a U.S. Department of State-designated Sponsor and does not issue Form DS-2019. We do not guarantee a job, hours, earnings, housing, visa or admission to the United States. Eligibility, programme acceptance and DS-2019 issuance are controlled by the designated Sponsor; visa and entry decisions are made by U.S. authorities. OTC provides education information, application preparation and coordination, not U.S. immigration legal advice. Parent updates for adult participants require the participant's authorisation.
