Overseas Publishing House

Ebook Publishing Support

面向教育機構、研究團隊、專業作者與公共文化項目的數字出版製作服務，涵蓋編輯結構整理、雙語版式設計、PDF / ebook 出版包、封面系統與平台上架元資料準備。

Service Positioning

From editorial manuscript to publication-grade digital edition.

OTC Overseas Publishing House supports disciplined editorial production for academic, educational and professional materials: structuring the manuscript, establishing a clear typographic system, preparing digital publication files and documenting the release workflow.

01Manuscript reviewCheck manuscript type, language, length, image/table needs, target reader and intended platform.
02Structure & editing plan整理章節、前言、目錄、作者簡介、版權頁、免責聲明與必要的中英文出版信息。
03Layout samplePrepare a short sample layout so the author can confirm typography, spacing, heading hierarchy and bilingual style.
04Ebook productionPrepare the full PDF / ebook package with consistent typography, page structure, cover placement and export settings.
05Listing assetsDraft product title, subtitle, description, category notes, author bio, preview image and Payhip / KDP upload checklist.
06Publication handoverProvide final files, change notes and a simple launch checklist for pricing, preview, test purchase and announcement.
What Authors Can Send First

A small document pack is enough to start.

Current manuscript

Word, Google Doc, Markdown or PDF draft. Half-written manuscripts are acceptable for first-stage planning.

Required

Book intention

Tell us whether the book is educational, practical, memoir, research, bilingual, workbook, guidebook or another format.

Required

Reference style

Any sample books, typography preferences, cover ideas or platform examples that show the intended reader experience.

Useful

Timeline and platform

Expected launch date and whether the author wants Payhip, Amazon KDP, PDF private sale or another ebook route.

Useful
Scope

What OTC can help with

  • Book structure, chapter hierarchy and reader flow
  • Chinese / English / bilingual PDF layout
  • Cover direction, title page, copyright page and imprint notes
  • Payhip product description and sales-page copy
  • KDP-ready checklist and guide file preparation support
  • Basic publication workflow and post-launch update planning
Boundaries

What this service is not

  • It is not a guaranteed bestseller or guaranteed sales service.
  • It is not legal advice, tax advice or copyright dispute representation.
  • It does not replace professional proofreading where a book needs formal copyediting.
  • It does not promise platform approval where Payhip, KDP or another platform applies its own rules.
Overseas Publishing House provides editorial production and digital-publication preparation services. Final content responsibility, rights clearance, institutional approvals and platform decisions remain with the author, commissioning body or rights holder.
