# Chapter 19

## Casebook A：Priority 與 Delegation 中文案例拆解

### 19.1 如何使用本案例卷

本章不是為了增加題量，而是示範如何把一道題拆成可學習的結構。每個案例都按同一順序：題幹任務、關鍵線索、選項陷阱、最佳答案理由、錯題標籤、OSCE 轉換。讀者應先遮住答案，用自己的話說出「我為什麼會選這個」，再看解析。

### 19.2 Case 1：術後呼吸困難

Stem:

A client is 6 hours postoperative after abdominal surgery. The client reports incisional pain 5/10, appears restless, and says, "I feel short of breath." Oxygen saturation is 89% on room air. Which action should the nurse take first?

Options:

A. Encourage the client to use a pillow to splint the incision.  
B. Assess respiratory status and apply oxygen according to protocol.  
C. Document the pain score and reassess in 30 minutes.  
D. Teach the client about expected postoperative discomfort.

中文拆解：

這題故意放入 postoperative pain，讓你想選疼痛處理或咳嗽支托。但題幹真正控制 priority 的 cue 是 restless、short of breath、SpO2 89%。這是 breathing/oxygenation 問題。A 可能是稍後有用的護理行動，但不能優先於低氧。C 和 D 都延誤了急性問題。

Best answer: B.

錯題標籤：

- cue_recognition
- prioritization
- safety

OSCE 轉換：

I am concerned about impaired oxygenation. I would assess respiratory status, check vital signs and oxygen saturation, position the client appropriately, apply oxygen according to protocol, escalate as needed, and reassess response.

### 19.3 Case 2：四位病人先看誰

Stem:

The nurse receives reports on four clients. Which client should the nurse assess first?

A. A client with chronic back pain requesting a heat pack.  
B. A client with pneumonia whose oxygen saturation dropped from 95% to 90%.  
C. A client with diabetes asking about meal choices.  
D. A client scheduled for discharge waiting for transport.

中文拆解：

四個病人都有需要，但 B 有 deterioration：oxygen saturation dropped。不是單一數字，而是趨勢變差。pneumonia + SpO2下降 指向 oxygenation risk。A 是 comfort，C 是 education，D 是 logistics。這題的核心是 trend + respiratory safety。

Best answer: B.

復盤句：

我不能把所有 request 都看成同等重要。priority 題要先找 unstable 或 worsening。

### 19.4 Case 3：委派給 UAP

Stem:

Which task is appropriate for the RN to delegate to unlicensed assistive personnel?

A. Collect a urine specimen from a stable client.  
B. Assess a newly admitted client with abdominal pain.  
C. Teach a client how to use an incentive spirometer for the first time.  
D. Evaluate whether a client's dizziness improved after medication.

中文拆解：

A 是穩定病人的標本收集，通常可在指導下委派。B 是 assessment，C 是 initial teaching，D 是 evaluation，都需要 RN judgment。委派題要看 task 是否 routine、stable、predictable，也要看是否需要 interpretation。

Best answer: A.

錯題標籤：

- delegation_scope
- RN_judgment

### 19.5 Case 4：正確但不優先

Stem:

A client admitted with heart failure reports shortness of breath while lying flat. The nurse notes crackles bilaterally. Which action is the priority?

A. Review sodium restriction teaching.  
B. Assist the client to sit upright.  
C. Ask about usual sleep habits.  
D. Provide written discharge instructions.

中文拆解：

Heart failure 病人有 dyspnea lying flat + crackles，提示 fluid/oxygenation concern。坐起來可改善呼吸，是更即時的 action。A 和 D 重要但不是 priority；C 可能與 orthopnea assessment 有關，但目前已有呼吸症狀和 crackles，應先幫助呼吸。

Best answer: B.

復盤句：

教育很重要，但當病人正在呼吸困難時，priority 是改善 breathing。

### 19.6 Case 5：跌倒風險與安全

Stem:

An older adult client started a sedating medication and tries to get out of bed without assistance. What should the nurse do first?

A. Remind the client that falls are dangerous.  
B. Place needed items within reach and activate fall precautions.  
C. Document that the client is noncompliant.  
D. Ask the family to visit later.

中文拆解：

sedating medication + older adult + tries to get out of bed 是 fall risk。A 是教育但不夠；C 帶有評判且不處理安全；D 不直接解決當前風險。B 直接降低 harm。

Best answer: B.

OSCE 轉換：

I would implement fall precautions, ensure the call bell and personal items are within reach, assess sedation and mobility, and communicate the risk to the care team.

### 19.7 Case 6：誰不能委派

Stem:

The RN works with an experienced UAP. Which task must remain with the RN?

A. Recording intake and output for a stable client.  
B. Ambulating a stable client after routine surgery.  
C. Teaching a client with a new diagnosis about medication safety.  
D. Assisting a client with bathing.

中文拆解：

experienced UAP 是干擾資訊。再有經驗，也不能接手需要 RN teaching 的新診斷用藥安全教育。A、B、D 都可能是 routine assistance 或 observation；C 需要 teaching、assessment of understanding 和 clinical judgment。

Best answer: C.

錯題標籤：

- delegation_scope
- teaching
- english_wording: must remain

### 19.8 Case 7：新症狀不能當常規任務

Stem:

Which client should not be assigned to an LPN/LVN without further RN assessment?

A. A stable client receiving routine oral antibiotics.  
B. A client who reports new chest tightness.  
C. A client requiring a dressing change on a healing wound.  
D. A client needing reinforcement of previously taught crutch walking.

中文拆解：

這題問 should not be assigned without further RN assessment。new chest tightness 是 acute/new symptom，需要 RN assessment。其他選項可能在範圍內或較 routine，具體仍看地區與機構政策。題目核心是 new/unstable cue。

Best answer: B.

復盤句：

只要出現 new symptom，我要先懷疑是否需要 RN assessment。

### 19.9 Case 8：先評估還是先處置

Stem:

A client reports dizziness after receiving a new antihypertensive medication. What should the nurse do first?

A. Help the client ambulate to assess tolerance.  
B. Check blood pressure and assess for orthostatic changes.  
C. Tell the client dizziness is expected.  
D. Document the report and continue routine care.

中文拆解：

new medication + dizziness 可能與 hypotension 或 orthostatic change 有關。第一步應評估安全和血壓。A 可能增加跌倒風險；C 過度安慰；D 不處理風險。這題不是立即通知 provider，而是先完成 RN assessment，除非題幹提供更危急資訊。

Best answer: B.

錯題標籤：

- assessment_before_action
- safety
- medication

### 19.10 Casebook A 總結

Priority 和 delegation 題的共同點，是它們都不只問知識。它們問你能不能在情境中辨識穩定性、急迫性、RN judgment 和 harm。每次錯這類題，都要問：我把哪個 cue 看輕了？我把哪個 routine action 看重了？我是否忽略了 first、priority、delegate、assign、must remain 這些題幹詞？
