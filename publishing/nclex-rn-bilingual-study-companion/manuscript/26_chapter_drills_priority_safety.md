# Chapter 26

## Chapter Drills A：Priority, Safety, Delegation

### 26.1 使用方式

本章是短題練習卷。每題後面不是長篇講義，而是標準復盤：key cue、best answer、why not、wrong tags。學員應先獨立作答，再用復盤表記錄。督導班可以每次選 5 題討論，不必一次做完。

### 26.2 Drill 1：Shortness of breath

Question:

The nurse receives four reports. Which client should be assessed first?

A. A client with chronic knee pain requesting an ice pack.  
B. A client with new shortness of breath and oxygen saturation of 90%.  
C. A client asking when discharge papers will be ready.  
D. A client who missed a routine stool softener.

Best answer: B.

Key cue: new shortness of breath + low oxygen saturation.

Why not: A is comfort, C is discharge logistics, D is routine medication issue unless more serious cues appear.

Wrong tags: cue_recognition, prioritization, safety.

中文復盤：看到 new + breathing + SpO2，就不能把它放在 routine request 後面。

### 26.3 Drill 2：Delegation

Question:

Which task is best to delegate to UAP?

A. Reinforce a complex discharge plan.  
B. Assess a client reporting new chest pressure.  
C. Assist a stable client with hygiene care.  
D. Evaluate whether nausea medication was effective.

Best answer: C.

Key cue: stable client + routine hygiene care.

Why not: A involves teaching complexity, B requires assessment, D requires evaluation.

Wrong tags: delegation_scope, RN_judgment.

中文復盤：UAP 可以協助穩定病人的例行照護，但不能替 RN 做首次評估和療效評價。

### 26.4 Drill 3：Fall risk

Question:

An older client taking a sedating medication tries to walk to the bathroom alone. What should the nurse do first?

A. Apply fall precautions and assist the client safely.  
B. Tell the client not to be difficult.  
C. Document noncompliance.  
D. Wait for family to arrive.

Best answer: A.

Key cue: older adult + sedating medication + unassisted ambulation.

Wrong tags: safety, prioritization.

中文復盤：跌倒風險題先降低 harm，不是先責備或只記錄。

### 26.5 Drill 4：Assessment before teaching

Question:

A client newly reports dizziness after the first dose of an antihypertensive. What is the nurse's first action?

A. Teach the client to avoid salt.  
B. Check blood pressure and assess for orthostatic changes.  
C. Encourage the client to walk in the hallway.  
D. Tell the client the symptom is expected.

Best answer: B.

Key cue: first dose + dizziness.

Why not: C may increase fall risk, D minimizes a possible safety issue, A is not first.

Wrong tags: medication_safety, assessment_before_action.

### 26.6 Drill 5：Bleeding

Question:

A postoperative client has a dressing saturated with bright red blood. What should the nurse do first?

A. Reinforce discharge teaching.  
B. Assess the site and vital signs, and escalate according to protocol.  
C. Give the client a snack.  
D. Document as expected drainage.

Best answer: B.

Key cue: saturated bright red blood.

Wrong tags: bleeding, safety, prioritization.

中文復盤：出血不是文件問題，是 circulation/safety 問題。

### 26.7 Drill 6：UAP reporting

Question:

The UAP reports that a client is suddenly confused. What should the RN do?

A. Ask the UAP to reassess in one hour.  
B. Assess the client promptly.  
C. Document normal aging.  
D. Tell the family to visit.

Best answer: B.

Key cue: suddenly confused.

Why not: sudden confusion may indicate deterioration, hypoxia, infection, medication effect or neuro change.

Wrong tags: cue_recognition, safety.

### 26.8 Drill 7：Correct but not first

Question:

A client with pneumonia has fever, increased work of breathing and SpO2 89%. Which action is priority?

A. Teach the client about completing antibiotics.  
B. Address oxygenation and assess respiratory status.  
C. Provide a brochure about pneumonia.  
D. Ask about home support.

Best answer: B.

Key cue: increased work of breathing + SpO2 89%.

Why not: Teaching and discharge planning matter later.

中文復盤：正確但不優先，是 priority 題最常見陷阱。

### 26.9 Drill 8：RN must keep

Question:

Which task should the RN keep rather than delegate?

A. Measuring intake and output.  
B. Transporting a stable client.  
C. Initial teaching for a new insulin prescription.  
D. Assisting with feeding for a stable client.

Best answer: C.

Key cue: initial teaching + new prescription.

Wrong tags: delegation_scope, teaching.

### 26.10 Drill 9：Aspiration

Question:

A client coughs repeatedly after drinking water following a stroke. What should the nurse do first?

A. Continue fluids slowly.  
B. Stop oral intake and assess swallowing safety.  
C. Offer a straw.  
D. Document dislike of water.

Best answer: B.

Key cue: stroke + coughing with water.

Wrong tags: aspiration, safety.

### 26.11 Drill 10：Escalation

Question:

A client with infection has new confusion, blood pressure 86/50 mmHg and heart rate 124/min. What is the priority?

A. Recognize deterioration and escalate care promptly.  
B. Provide routine teaching.  
C. Offer a warm blanket only.  
D. Recheck in four hours.

Best answer: A.

Key cue: infection + hypotension + tachycardia + new confusion.

Wrong tags: sepsis, safety, prioritization.

### 26.12 本章總結

這 10 題的共同點，是它們都要求你先找 harm。若你的錯題集中在 B 類答案上，可能代表你知道護理常規，但還沒有穩定地把 acute safety cue 放在第一位。
