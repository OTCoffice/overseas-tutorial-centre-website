# Chapter 20

## Casebook B：藥理、安全與感染控制案例拆解

### 20.1 使用方式

本章聚焦高風險題：藥物不良反應、出血、低氧、感染控制、跌倒、誤吸、腎功能和管路安全。這些題不應只用記憶處理。每一題都要問：最可能造成 harm 的是什麼？RN 能立即做什麼？什麼時候需要 notify 或 escalate？

### 20.2 Case 1：Warfarin and bleeding

Stem:

A client taking warfarin reports black, tarry stools. Which action is most appropriate?

A. Encourage increased intake of leafy green vegetables.  
B. Tell the client this is an expected effect.  
C. Assess for bleeding and notify the provider according to protocol.  
D. Advise the client to take aspirin for discomfort.

中文拆解：

warfarin + black tarry stools 指向 possible gastrointestinal bleeding。A 涉及飲食穩定性，但不處理當前危險；B 錯誤；D 增加 bleeding risk。C 回應出血風險。

Best answer: C.

復盤句：

抗凝題先問 bleeding harm，不要只背 INR 或飲食。

### 20.3 Case 2：Gentamicin and ototoxicity

Stem:

A client receiving gentamicin reports ringing in the ears. What should the nurse do?

A. Document it as an expected harmless effect.  
B. Hold the medication and notify the provider according to protocol.  
C. Encourage the client to drink more fluids only.  
D. Reassure the client that hearing changes are temporary.

中文拆解：

gentamicin 屬 aminoglycoside，ringing in ears 可提示 ototoxicity。題目不是問普通耳鳴處理，而是藥物安全。B 是較安全方向，具體行動受醫囑與機構流程影響。

Best answer: B.

錯題標籤：

- pharmacology
- toxicity
- safety

### 20.4 Case 3：Opioid and respiratory depression

Stem:

A postoperative client received morphine. The client is difficult to arouse and has a respiratory rate of 8/min. What is the priority?

A. Encourage oral fluids.  
B. Assess airway and breathing and prepare to intervene per protocol.  
C. Teach the client about pain management.  
D. Document that the client is sleeping.

中文拆解：

morphine + difficult to arouse + RR 8/min 是 respiratory depression 風險。這是 priority safety issue。C 是稍後教育；D 危險地淡化；A 無關當前危險。

Best answer: B.

復盤句：

藥理題看到 CNS/respiratory depression cues，要先處理 airway/breathing。

### 20.5 Case 4：Lithium toxicity

Stem:

A client taking lithium reports vomiting, diarrhea, and increasing tremor. Which concern is most important?

A. The client may be dehydrated and developing lithium toxicity.  
B. The client needs routine dietary teaching only.  
C. These are expected effects that do not require action.  
D. The medication should be doubled after vomiting.

中文拆解：

lithium 安全重點是 toxicity 和 fluid/sodium balance。vomiting、diarrhea、tremor 不是可忽略背景。A 抓住主要風險。D 明顯危險。

Best answer: A.

OSCE 轉換：

I am concerned about possible lithium toxicity, especially with fluid loss. I would assess symptoms, vital signs, hydration status, medication history, and escalate according to protocol.

### 20.6 Case 5：Aspiration risk after stroke

Stem:

A client admitted after a stroke is coughing when offered water. What should the nurse do first?

A. Continue offering water slowly.  
B. Stop oral intake and assess swallowing risk according to protocol.  
C. Tell the family to bring softer food.  
D. Document that the client dislikes water.

中文拆解：

stroke + coughing with water 指向 aspiration risk。第一步不是鼓勵喝，也不是換軟食，而是停止口服並評估吞嚥安全。B 最安全。

Best answer: B.

錯題標籤：

- safety
- aspiration
- cue_recognition

### 20.7 Case 6：Airborne precautions

Stem:

The nurse suspects a client has an airborne infection. Which action is appropriate?

A. Use standard precautions only.  
B. Place the client in an appropriate airborne infection isolation room and use respiratory protection per policy.  
C. Keep the door open for ventilation.  
D. Ask the client to share a room with another coughing client.

中文拆解：

airborne infection 需要按 airborne precautions 和機構政策處理。A 不完整，C/D 不安全。B 是合理方向。具體病種和政策需按官方/機構要求。

Best answer: B.

復盤句：

感染控制先問 transmission route，不要只選 generic PPE。

### 20.8 Case 7：AV fistula protection

Stem:

A client with an arteriovenous fistula for hemodialysis has no palpable thrill. What should the nurse do?

A. Notify the provider promptly and protect the fistula arm.  
B. Take blood pressure on that arm to confirm circulation.  
C. Draw blood from the fistula arm.  
D. Tell the client this is expected.

中文拆解：

absent thrill/bruit 可能提示 fistula occlusion。要保護 fistula arm，不應在該手臂測血壓或抽血。A 最安全。

Best answer: A.

錯題標籤：

- renal
- safety
- access_protection

### 20.9 Case 8：Sepsis cue recognition

Stem:

A client with a urinary tract infection becomes confused. Temperature is 39°C, heart rate 118/min, and blood pressure 88/54 mmHg. What is the priority?

A. Provide routine discharge teaching.  
B. Recognize possible sepsis and escalate care promptly.  
C. Encourage the client to sleep.  
D. Ask the family if confusion is normal.

中文拆解：

infection + fever + tachycardia + hypotension + confusion 是 systemic deterioration。這不是普通 UTI 教育題。B 抓住可能 sepsis 和 escalation。D 可作背景，但不能延誤 priority。

Best answer: B.

復盤句：

感染題一旦出現 abnormal vitals 和 mental status change，要從 local infection 升級思考 systemic risk。

### 20.10 Casebook B 總結

藥理和安全題的共同核心是 harm。不要只問「這個藥做什麼」，要問「它最怕什麼危險」。不要只問「感染要不要戴手套」，要問「它如何傳播」。不要只問「這個症狀常不常見」，要問「它是否提示惡化」。這種 harm-first 思維，是華語考生從知識背誦轉向 NCLEX clinical judgment 的關鍵。
