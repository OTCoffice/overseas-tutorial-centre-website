# Chapter 27

## Chapter Drills B：Pharmacology, Maternal-Child, Mental Health

### 27.1 使用方式

本章題目聚焦高風險內容。若用於公開產品，題目和 rationale 必須經 RN / nursing educator review。本章目前作為內部教學草稿，重點在展示復盤方式。

### 27.2 Drill 1：Warfarin

Question:

Which statement by a client taking warfarin requires further teaching?

A. "I will report unusual bleeding."  
B. "I will keep my blood test appointments."  
C. "I will take aspirin whenever I have a headache."  
D. "I will check with my provider before new medicines."

Best answer: C.

Key wording: requires further teaching.

Rationale: aspirin can increase bleeding risk and should not be self-started without professional guidance.

Wrong tags: english_wording, pharmacology, safety.

### 27.3 Drill 2：Gentamicin

Question:

A client receiving gentamicin reports ringing in the ears. What is the priority concern?

A. Possible ototoxicity.  
B. Expected hydration response.  
C. Improved infection control.  
D. Normal sleep pattern.

Best answer: A.

Key cue: ringing in ears.

Wrong tags: pharmacology, toxicity.

### 27.4 Drill 3：Opioid

Question:

A client who received an opioid is difficult to arouse and has respirations of 7/min. What should the nurse do first?

A. Encourage ambulation.  
B. Assess airway and breathing and intervene per protocol.  
C. Offer oral fluids.  
D. Document that the client is resting.

Best answer: B.

Key cue: difficult to arouse + RR 7/min.

Wrong tags: respiratory_depression, safety.

### 27.8 Drill 7：Postpartum

Question:

A postpartum client has heavy lochia and a boggy uterus. What is the expected immediate NCLEX-style action after assessment confirms atony?

A. Fundal massage per protocol.  
B. Discharge teaching.

D. Routine documentation only.

Best answer: A.

Safety note: real clinical action follows facility protocol and provider/midwifery guidance.

Wrong tags: bleeding, maternal_safety.

### 27.11 Drill 10：Delusion

### 27.12 本章總結

高風險題的重點不是背答案，而是建立安全語言。藥理題問 harm，產兒題問母嬰穩定，精神健康題問安全與治療性溝通。每題都應能轉成一句中文復盤和一句英文安全表達。
