# Chapter 23

## NGN Long Case Workshop：長病例逐步拆解

### 23.1 本章定位

NGN case study 對華語考生的壓力，不只是英文長，而是資料會分批出現，題目會要求你在不同階段做不同判斷。短題常能靠單一 cue 解出，長病例則要看時間線、趨勢和前後關係。本章用工作坊方式示範如何讀長病例。每個 case 都不是官方題，也不是仿真承諾，而是教你如何復盤 clinical judgment。

### 23.2 Long Case Reading Protocol

每個長病例先做四步：

1. Background：病人是誰，主要情境是什麼。
2. Cues：哪些資料異常、變化或與安全有關。
3. Hypotheses：可能的主要風險是什麼。
4. Actions and evaluation：現在做什麼，如何知道有效。

不要一開始就看所有選項。選項會帶你走。先建立自己的判斷，再看選項哪一個最符合。

### 23.3 Case 1：Postoperative respiratory deterioration

Background:

A 58-year-old client is 8 hours postoperative after abdominal surgery. The client has an IV line, reports pain 4/10, and has been sleeping intermittently.

New data:

- Respiratory rate 30/min.
- Oxygen saturation 88% on room air.
- Restless and says, "I cannot get enough air."
- Incision dressing dry.
- Blood pressure 132/78 mmHg.

Step 1 Recognize cues:

關鍵 cues 是 RR 30、SpO2 88%、restless、cannot get enough air。Pain 4/10 和 dry dressing 是背景資訊，不是此刻 priority。

Step 2 Analyze cues:

這些資料指向 breathing/oxygenation concern。術後病人有呼吸惡化，需要立即護理評估與處理。

Step 3 Prioritize hypotheses:

最優先 hypothesis 是 impaired oxygenation。不是 pain control，不是 discharge teaching，不是 routine documentation。

Step 4 Actions:

Assess respiratory status, position appropriately, apply oxygen according to protocol, notify/escalate as needed, reassess SpO2 and work of breathing.

中文復盤：

這個 case 的陷阱是 postoperative pain。疼痛重要，但題目給了客觀低氧和呼吸急促。NCLEX 中，客觀氧合危險通常優先於舒適。

### 23.4 Case 1 Mini Questions

Question A: Which cues are most important?

Best cues: RR 30/min, SpO2 88%, restlessness, statement of shortness of breath.

Question B: Which action is least appropriate as first action?

Least appropriate: provide discharge teaching or wait to reassess later.

Question C: What outcome should be evaluated?

Expected evaluation: improved oxygen saturation, reduced work of breathing, improved restlessness, stable vital signs.

### 23.5 Case 2：Medication safety and bleeding

Background:

A client with atrial fibrillation takes warfarin. The client is preparing for discharge.

Client statements:

- "I will report black stools."
- "I will keep my lab appointments."
- "If I have a headache, I will take aspirin because it is over the counter."
- "I will tell my provider before starting new medicines."

Step 1 Recognize cues:

關鍵 cue 是 aspirin because it is over the counter。OTC 不代表安全。Warfarin + aspirin 可增加 bleeding risk。

Step 2 Analyze cues:

這是 patient teaching 題。題目若問 further teaching，應找 unsafe statement。

Step 3 Prioritize:

最重要不是背所有 warfarin teaching，而是辨識哪句話會造成 harm。

Step 4 Action:

Provide further teaching about avoiding aspirin/NSAIDs unless directed, bleeding signs, lab monitoring and provider communication.

中文復盤：

這個 case 的關鍵是 wording。若題目問 needs further teaching，答案是錯誤陳述，而不是正確陳述。

### 23.6 Case 3：Mental health crisis

Background:

A client admitted for depression sits alone and says, "Everyone would be better without me."

Additional data:

- The client has stopped attending group therapy.
- Appetite decreased.
- Gives away personal items.
- Says, "I have thought about taking all my pills."

Step 1 Recognize cues:

最重要 cues 是 suicidal statement、giving away items、specific pill plan。這不是普通 sadness。

Step 2 Analyze cues:

這是 self-harm risk，需要直接 safety assessment 和 escalation。

Step 3 Prioritize:

安全優先於一般 therapeutic exploration。不能只說 "Tell me more about your hobbies" 或 "Your family loves you."

Step 4 Action:

Ask directly about suicidal intent, plan, means; stay with client as appropriate; remove hazards per policy; notify mental health team/provider; document and follow facility protocol.

中文復盤：

精神科題的安全線索不能被安慰語句掩蓋。直接問自傷風險是安全評估，不是冒犯。

### 23.7 Case 4：Maternal-child deterioration

Background:

A postpartum client delivered 2 hours ago. The client reports dizziness.

Assessment:

- Heavy lochia.
- Boggy uterus.
- Heart rate 118/min.
- Blood pressure 92/58 mmHg.
- Skin cool and clammy.

Step 1 Recognize cues:

heavy lochia、boggy uterus、tachycardia、hypotension、cool clammy skin 都指向 bleeding/perfusion concern。

Step 2 Analyze cues:

可能 postpartum hemorrhage / uterine atony context。

Step 3 Prioritize:

出血與 circulation 風險優先。不能先做 routine teaching、shower、ambulation。

Step 4 Action:

Assess fundus and bleeding, initiate immediate nursing actions for suspected atony/bleeding per protocol, call for help/escalate, monitor vital signs and response.

中文復盤：

本書避免把單一動作寫成所有臨床情境的固定反射。考試中若明確 boggy uterus with heavy lochia，fundal massage 常是 expected action；真實實務仍需 assessment、protocol 和團隊指引。

### 23.8 Case 5：Sepsis risk

Background:

A client is being treated for pneumonia.

New data:

- Temperature 39.2°C.
- Heart rate 124/min.
- Blood pressure 86/50 mmHg.
- New confusion.
- Skin cool.

Step 1 Recognize cues:

infection + abnormal vitals + hypotension + new confusion。

Step 2 Analyze cues:

Possible sepsis / shock concern.

Step 3 Prioritize:

This is systemic deterioration, not routine infection teaching.

Step 4 Action:

Rapid assessment, escalate care, follow sepsis-related facility protocol, monitor perfusion/oxygenation, prepare ordered interventions.

中文復盤：

感染題若只有 local symptoms，可以按感染控制或教育處理；若出現 hypotension、tachycardia、confusion，思路要升級到 systemic risk。

### 23.10 本章總結

NGN 長病例的學習目標不是讀得快，而是讀得有結構。你要知道哪些資料是背景，哪些資料改變 priority，哪些資料用來 evaluate outcome。當你能把長病例壓縮成 cue-risk-action-evaluation 四格，NGN 就不再只是長，而是可處理。
