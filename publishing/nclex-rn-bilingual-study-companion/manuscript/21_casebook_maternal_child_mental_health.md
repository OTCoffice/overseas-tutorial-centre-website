# Chapter 21

## Casebook C：產兒、精神健康與溝通案例拆解

### 21.1 本章定位

產兒和精神健康題常讓考生覺得陌生，但它們的底層仍是 safety、cue recognition、therapeutic communication 和 priority。不要把產兒題只當成週數記憶，也不要把精神科題只當成語氣選擇。本章用案例展示如何用安全線索讀題。

### 21.2 Case 1：Preeclampsia warning signs

Stem:

A pregnant client reports severe headache and blurred vision. What is the nurse's priority?

A. Reassure the client that headaches are common in pregnancy.  
B. Assess blood pressure and notify the provider according to protocol.  
C. Encourage the client to rest in a dark room only.  
D. Ask the client to drink more water and call back tomorrow.

中文拆解：

severe headache + blurred vision 在 pregnancy 中不是普通不適，可能提示 preeclampsia concern。B 是安全方向。A/C/D 都有淡化危險的問題。

Best answer: B.

錯題標籤：

- maternal_safety
- cue_recognition
- prioritization

### 21.3 Case 2：Late decelerations

Stem:

During labor, the fetal monitor shows recurrent late decelerations. What should the nurse do first?

A. Continue routine observation.  
B. Reposition the client and initiate intrauterine resuscitation measures per protocol.  
C. Encourage the client to push harder.  
D. Document the strip at the end of the shift.

中文拆解：

recurrent late decelerations 指向 uteroplacental perfusion / fetal oxygenation concern。行動通常包括 reposition、停止 oxytocin if applicable、IV fluids、oxygen/notification per protocol 等，具體按題幹與流程。B 最接近安全方向。

Best answer: B.

復盤句：

胎心監測題不是背名詞，而是判斷 fetal oxygenation 是否受威脅。

### 21.4 Case 3：Postpartum hemorrhage cue

Stem:

A postpartum client has heavy lochia and a boggy uterus. Which action is expected first in this NCLEX-style question?

A. Encourage the client to ambulate.  
B. Massage the fundus after assessment confirms uterine atony and follow protocol.  
C. Provide discharge teaching.  
D. Document as expected.

中文拆解：

heavy lochia + boggy uterus 指向 uterine atony and bleeding risk。NCLEX-style 題通常期待 fundal massage 作為 immediate action，但本書保留安全措辭：assessment confirms atony，follow protocol。D 錯誤，C 不優先，A 可能不安全。

Best answer: B.

錯題標籤：

- maternal_safety
- bleeding
- prioritization

### 21.5 Case 4：Newborn respiratory distress

Stem:

A newborn has grunting, nasal flaring, and retractions. What should the nurse do?

A. Continue routine feeding.  
B. Recognize respiratory distress and escalate care promptly.  
C. Tell the parents this is expected.  
D. Delay assessment until the next scheduled vital signs.

中文拆解：

grunting、nasal flaring、retractions 都是 newborn respiratory distress cues。新生兒呼吸問題不能等。B 是安全方向。

Best answer: B.

復盤句：

新生兒題看到呼吸費力線索，要優先於 routine feeding 或一般安慰。

### 21.6 Case 5：Suicidal ideation

Stem:

A client says, "I have been thinking about ending my life." Which response is most appropriate?

A. "You have many reasons to live."  
B. "Are you thinking about harming yourself now?"  
C. "Let's talk about your hobbies."  
D. "You should not upset your family."

中文拆解：

這是 suicidal ideation 安全評估。B 直接、清楚、非評判。A 是 false reassurance/勸說，C 轉移話題，D 帶壓力與評判。

Best answer: B.

OSCE 轉換：

I would ask directly about suicidal thoughts, plan, means and immediate safety, stay with the client as needed, remove hazards according to policy, and escalate to the mental health team.

### 21.7 Case 6：Delusion communication

Stem:

A client says, "The cameras in the ceiling are watching me." Which response is therapeutic?

A. "That is impossible."  
B. "I do not see cameras, but I can see this feels frightening to you."  
C. "You are being silly."  
D. "If you behave, the cameras will stop."

中文拆解：

治療性溝通不與 delusion 爭辯，也不強化妄想。B 表示不認同妄想內容，但承認感受。A 爭辯，C 評判，D 強化妄想。

Best answer: B.

錯題標籤：

- therapeutic_communication
- mental_health

### 21.8 Case 7：Panic communication

Stem:

A client is pacing, breathing rapidly, and says, "I am losing control." Which nursing response is best?

A. Give detailed explanations about anxiety physiology.  
B. Stay with the client and use short, calm statements.  
C. Ask the client to complete a long questionnaire.  
D. Tell the client to calm down immediately.

中文拆解：

panic 狀態下，長篇教育或複雜任務不合適。B 支持安全、降低刺激、保持簡短。D 命令式無效且可能增加焦慮。

Best answer: B.

復盤句：

溝通題要看病人當下能處理多少資訊，不是把所有正確知識一次說完。

### 21.9 Case 8：Mania safety

Stem:

A client with mania is loud, intrusive, and unable to sit through meals. What is an appropriate nursing intervention?

A. Provide a low-stimulation environment and portable nutritious snacks.  
B. Encourage complex group discussion.  
C. Confront the client in front of peers.  
D. Remove all food until the client sits quietly.

中文拆解：

mania 題要看 safety、nutrition、low stimulation、brief clear directions。A 合理。B/C/D 都可能增加刺激或不安全。

Best answer: A.

錯題標籤：

- mental_health
- safety
- communication

### 21.10 Casebook C 總結

產兒題的核心是母嬰安全，精神健康題的核心是安全與治療性溝通。這兩類題都容易被情緒化處理：看到孕婦頭痛就安慰，看到自殺語句就勸說，看到妄想就反駁。但 NCLEX-RN 期待的是結構化安全判斷。先問：誰有 harm risk？哪個 cue 不能被淡化？哪句話支持安全和治療性關係？
