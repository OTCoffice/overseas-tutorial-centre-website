# Chapter 22

## Extended Glossary and Phrasebook：雙語術語與口頭表達

### 22.1 本章定位

詞彙不是孤立背誦。對 NCLEX-RN 和 OSCE 轉換來說，詞彙必須放在句子和情境中。clinical judgment、priority、cue、hypothesis、intervention、evaluation、escalation 這些詞不是裝飾，它們是你理解題目和表達護理判斷的骨架。

### 22.2 Clinical Judgment Terms

| English | 中文理解 | 使用句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| cue | 線索 | The key cue is the oxygen saturation of 89%. |
| recognize cues | 辨識線索 | I need to recognize which data are clinically important. |
| analyze cues | 分析線索 | I would analyze whether the cue suggests deterioration. |
| prioritize hypotheses | 排序可能問題 | I would prioritize impaired oxygenation. |
| generate solutions | 產生處理方案 | Possible solutions include assessment, positioning and escalation. |
| take action | 採取行動 | My first action is to address breathing. |
| evaluate outcomes | 評估結果 | I would reassess oxygen saturation and work of breathing. |

### 22.3 Priority Terms

| English | 中文理解 | 注意 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| first | 最先 | 不等於唯一正確 |
| priority | 優先 | 找最急、最安全 |
| immediate | 立即 | 不能延誤 |
| urgent | 緊急 | 可能造成 harm |
| stable | 穩定 | 可預期，優先性較低 |
| unstable | 不穩定 | 急性變化或高風險 |
| acute | 急性 | new/sudden/worsening |
| chronic | 慢性 | 若穩定通常不優先 |

練習句：

- The client with new shortness of breath is unstable.
- The chronic pain report is important but not the first priority.
- The acute change requires immediate assessment.

### 22.4 Delegation Terms

| English | 中文理解 | 例句 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| delegate | 委派 | The RN may delegate routine tasks for stable clients. |
| supervise | 監督 | The RN remains responsible for supervision. |
| scope of practice | 執業範圍 | The task must match staff scope of practice. |
| assessment | 評估 | Initial assessment requires RN judgment. |
| teaching | 教學 | New teaching should remain with the RN. |
| evaluation | 評價 | Evaluation of response requires RN judgment. |

中文提醒：委派題最容易被「誰有空」或「誰有經驗」干擾。真正要看的是 stable、routine、predictable、scope。

### 22.5 Safety and Escalation Phrases

OSCE 和督導討論中，可練習以下句型：

- I am concerned about...
- My immediate priority is...
- I would assess...
- I would stop oral intake because...
- I would protect the fistula arm.
- I would notify the provider according to protocol.
- I would escalate care because the client is deteriorating.
- I would reassess after the intervention.
- I would document the findings and actions.

中文練習：每做錯一道 safety 題，用上面句型說一遍。不求流利，先求安全邏輯完整。

### 22.6 Patient Teaching Phrases

| Teaching phrase | 中文用途 |
| --- | --- |
| Report black stools or unusual bleeding. | 抗凝出血警訊 |
| Do not stop the medication suddenly unless instructed. | 避免自行停藥 |
| Check with your provider before taking new medications. | 避免交互作用 |
| Use the call bell before getting out of bed. | 跌倒預防 |
| Keep follow-up blood tests as scheduled. | 監測與安全 |

題目若問 needs further teaching，就找病人哪句話違反這些安全方向。

### 22.7 Communication Phrasebook

Therapeutic:

- Tell me more about what you are feeling.
- That sounds frightening.
- I can stay with you while we work through this.
- Are you thinking about harming yourself?
- I do not see that, but I understand it feels real to you.

Nontherapeutic:

- Everything will be fine.
- You should not feel that way.
- Why did you do that?
- Let's not talk about that.
- That is not true, stop saying it.

中文提醒：治療性溝通不是「說得漂亮」，而是支持安全、表達和信任。

### 22.8 Infection Control Phrasebook

- Use standard precautions for all clients.
- Identify the route of transmission.
- Use airborne precautions as indicated.
- Use droplet precautions as indicated.
- Use contact precautions as indicated.
- Perform hand hygiene before and after client contact.
- Dedicate equipment when required.

中文提醒：看到 infection control 題，先問傳播途徑，再選 PPE 和房間安排。

### 22.9 OSCE Mini Handover Template

SBAR:

Situation: I am concerned about...

Background: The client is...

Assessment: I noticed...

Recommendation: I recommend / I would like...

中文例：

Situation: 我擔心病人正在低氧。  
Background: 病人腹部手術後 6 小時。  
Assessment: 病人不安、呼吸困難、SpO2 89%。  
Recommendation: 我會立即評估呼吸、按流程給氧、通知團隊並重新評估。

英文例：

I am concerned about impaired oxygenation. The client is six hours postoperative, appears restless, reports shortness of breath and has an oxygen saturation of 89% on room air. I would assess respiratory status, intervene according to protocol, escalate to the team and reassess the response.

### 22.10 本章使用方式

不要試圖一次背完整章。每週從錯題中挑 10 個詞，放進自己的 glossary。每個詞必須有一個例句，最好來自你做錯的題。若一個詞沒有例句，就只是單字；若一個詞能放進錯題復盤，它才是考試語言。
