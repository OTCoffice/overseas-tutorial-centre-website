# Claude Review Request

Please review the attached PDF and manuscript as an external editorial reviewer for OTC / Overseas Publishing House.

## Project

Title: BTEC Level 3 Business Assignment Writing Toolkit  
Format: independent bilingual study-support companion  
Audience: Chinese / EAL learners studying BTEC Level 3 Business or similar assignment-based business courses  
Current length: 121 PDF pages; approximately 28,885 words in the markdown manuscript  
Status: expanded review draft v0.4

## Important positioning

This publication must be understood as an independent bilingual output-system resource for assignment-based learners.

It must not present itself as:

- an official Pearson / BTEC publication;
- an official guide;
- a marking guide;
- a model-answer book;
- a guaranteed-pass product;
- a substitute for centre-issued briefs, mark schemes, assessor guidance or teacher feedback.

The book should train:

- assignment brief reading;
- command-word interpretation;
- evidence planning;
- Pass / Merit / Distinction awareness without grade promises;
- paragraph structure;
- bilingual academic phrasing;
- academic integrity and AI-use boundaries;
- final submission self-checking.

## Review tasks

Please review the PDF and manuscript in four passes.

### 1. Content accuracy and educational logic

Check whether the manuscript explains BTEC Level 3 Business assignment writing in a way that is educationally sound, practical and appropriate for Chinese / EAL learners.

Flag:

- inaccurate or misleading statements about BTEC, Pearson, assignment-based learning or assessment;
- advice that may overpromise grades;
- places where the book sounds like it is replacing official centre guidance;
- weak, vague or repetitive learning points;
- chapters or tool sections that feel padded rather than useful.

### 2. Academic integrity and compliance

Check whether the wording creates risk around official affiliation, grade guarantee, model answers or academic misconduct.

Flag any phrase that should be softened, removed or rewritten.

Particular attention:

- “official”, “guide”, “marking”, “model answer”, “guarantee”, “submission-ready”;
- AI-use guidance;
- paragraph frames and phrase banks;
- Pass / Merit / Distinction discussion.

### 3. Bilingual quality

Check whether the English and Chinese function well together.

Flag:

- Chinese that is too intrusive or too large in conceptual weight;
- English that sounds unnatural, repetitive or AI-generated;
- Chinese explanations that do not add value;
- inconsistent treatment of English terms such as assignment brief, evidence, judgement, academic integrity and centre guidance.

### 4. PDF layout and publishing quality

Review the PDF as an A4 educational publishing product.

Flag:

- first-page / cover problems;
- duplicate headers or repeated title blocks;
- oversized headings;
- excessive blank space;
- crowded tables;
- poor page breaks;
- repetitive visual modules;
- footer issues;
- whether the colour system improves navigation or looks decorative;
- whether the current 121-page length feels justified after v0.4 structural cuts.

## Output format requested

Please return:

1. A short verdict: GREEN / AMBER / RED for publication readiness.
2. The top 10 must-fix issues, with page or section references where possible.
3. The top 10 nice-to-have improvements.
4. Any compliance wording that should be rewritten.
5. A brief recommendation on whether the manuscript should remain one volume or be split into workbook + phrasebook / templates.
6. A final “publisher’s decision note” of 150-250 words.

Please be strict. The goal is not encouragement; the goal is whether this can become a professional OTC Publishing House product.
