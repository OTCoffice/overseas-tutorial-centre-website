## Project Goal

To develop a premium bilingual BTEC Level 3 Business assignment-writing companion for Chinese EAL learners, helping students understand assignment briefs, plan evidence, write applied business paragraphs, improve Pass / Merit / Distinction awareness, and use AI or tutor support responsibly without breaching academic-integrity rules.

中文目標：打造一套面向中國 EAL 學生的高質感雙語 BTEC Level 3 Business assignment 寫作教輔，幫助學生讀懂 brief、拆解 command words、規劃 evidence、完成 applied business writing，理解 Pass / Merit / Distinction 的寫作差異，並在合規前提下使用 AI、教師與補習支持，避免學術不端風險。

## Publication Standard

The manuscript should be publication-ready only when it meets all the following conditions:

1. Clear independent-publication boundary: no official Pearson/BTEC claim, no official brief reproduction, no mark scheme reproduction, no guaranteed grade language.
2. Strong BTEC Business relevance: assignment briefs, command words, evidence planning, applied business context, Pass/Merit/Distinction awareness and academic integrity are central.
3. Chinese EAL usefulness: bilingual explanations are not decorative; they explain thinking traps, translation risks and English academic-writing moves.
4. Practical learner tools: checklists, planning grids, evidence logs, paragraph frames, phrase banks, final submission checks and tutor-safe support protocols.
5. Premium tone: not cheap tutoring notes, not a worksheet dump, not a model-answer booklet.
6. Product readiness: front matter, contents, compliance notice, glossary, appendix tools, product description and listing copy are complete.
