Sample Context

Practice brief: improving a small business marketing campaign.

The sample below demonstrates the intended publication style: concise English instruction, Chinese concept support, structured evidence planning and clear academic-integrity boundaries. The practice task is original OTC sample material and is not taken from any official Pearson / BTEC assignment brief.

Practice Assignment Scenario

Harbour Bean Cafe

Harbour Bean Cafe is an independent cafe near a railway station. It has steady weekday footfall but weak weekend sales. The owner wants to improve local student and family engagement through a low-budget social media campaign, loyalty card and partnership with a nearby bookstore.

Student task: prepare a short report that explains the current marketing problem, recommends a suitable promotional approach and evaluates the likely limits of the recommendation.

中文導讀

這不是要學生寫廣告文案。

本題的核心不是「想一個好看的宣傳活動」，而是訓練學生如何把企業問題、目標顧客、低成本渠道、證據與限制放進一個清楚的 business report。

學生需要證明自己理解 marketing decision，而不是只描述 cafe 很需要宣傳。

Sample Page 1

Assignment brief breakdown.

Brief wordingWhat the learner must do中文提示Evidence to prepare
Explain the current marketing problem.Identify the business issue, target customers and why the current approach is not enough.不是泛泛說「銷售不好」，而是指出週末客流、學生家庭客群、現有渠道不足。Sales pattern, customer observation, competitor comparison, local area notes.
Recommend a suitable promotional approach.Choose realistic marketing actions that fit budget, business size and customer group.建議要可執行，不能像大公司 campaign；要符合小店資源。Social media plan, loyalty card outline, partner bookstore idea, simple cost estimate.
Evaluate likely limits.Show what might reduce success and how the business could monitor results.Distinction 類寫法通常需要看到限制、風險、條件和衡量方式。Budget risk, staff time, weak data, seasonality, tracking method.
Sample Page 2

Pass / Merit / Distinction awareness without giving model answers.

Pass-level direction

Describe the business problem and explain one reasonable marketing response with basic evidence. The answer should be clear and relevant, but may remain mostly descriptive.

中文：先做到任務完整、概念正確、證據基本相關。

P

Merit-level direction

Apply evidence more carefully to the cafe. Compare more than one promotional option and explain why one option fits the business better.

中文：不能只背 marketing mix，要把證據真正放回企業情境。

M

Distinction-level direction

Evaluate trade-offs, limits and conditions. A stronger answer explains what could go wrong, what data is missing and how the owner should judge success.

中文：高分不是字數更多，而是判斷更成熟、限制更清楚。

D
Sample Page 3

Evidence planning matrix.

Claim to makeEvidence neededSource noteWriting use
Weekend sales are weaker than weekday sales.Simple weekly sales comparison or owner observation.Use internal business notes if available; do not invent exact numbers.Use to justify why the campaign targets weekend customers.
Students may respond to loyalty incentives.Local student footfall, competitor loyalty schemes, short customer survey.Keep the evidence modest; avoid unsupported claims about all students.Use to explain why loyalty cards may be suitable but limited.
A bookstore partnership could extend reach.Shared local customer group and low-cost cross-promotion example.Frame as a realistic option, not a guaranteed sales solution.Use in recommendation and evaluation paragraphs.
Mini Writing Frame

Evaluation paragraph skeleton

Point: The loyalty card may be suitable for Harbour Bean Cafe because ...

Evidence: This is supported by ...

Application: In this business, the evidence matters because ...

Limit: However, the recommendation may be limited by ...

Judgement: Therefore, the owner should ...

中文寫作提示

不要直接翻譯中文作文。

  • 用 evidence suggests that，而不是 I think。
  • 用 this may be suitable because，而不是 this is very good。
  • 用 however / therefore / this depends on 連接判斷。
  • 每段只處理一個清楚觀點，不要把所有想法堆在一起。
Sample Glossary

Vocabulary is taught as academic use, not just translation.

TermAcademic meaning中文理解Useful phrase
Target marketThe group of customers a business aims to reach.目標顧客群，不是所有可能買東西的人。The target market for this campaign is likely to be ...
PromotionCommunication activities used to inform or persuade customers.促銷/推廣，重點是與顧客溝通。A low-cost promotional method may be suitable because ...
EvaluationA reasoned judgement that considers strengths, limits and conditions.評估，不是簡單說優點和缺點。The effectiveness of this option depends on ...
