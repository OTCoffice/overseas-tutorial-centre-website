Sample Context

Practice brief: improving a small business marketing campaign.

The sample below demonstrates the intended publication style: concise English instruction, Chinese concept support, structured evidence planning and clear academic-integrity boundaries. The practice task is original OTC sample material created independently from Pearson / BTEC assignment briefs.

Practice Assignment Scenario

Harbour Bean Cafe

Harbour Bean Cafe is an independent cafe near a railway station. It has steady weekday footfall but weak weekend sales. The owner wants to improve local student and family engagement through a low-budget social media campaign, loyalty card and partnership with a nearby bookstore.

Student task: prepare a short report that explains the current marketing problem, recommends a suitable promotional approach and evaluates the likely limits of the recommendation.

中文導讀

本題訓練 business report 的輸出邏輯。

學生要把企業問題、目標顧客、低成本渠道、證據與限制放進一個清楚的 business report。

學生需要證明自己理解 marketing decision，並用證據說明 cafe 的宣傳需求與方案限制。

讀題順序：先圈出 business problem，再圈出 target customers，最後圈出 evaluate。這三個詞決定整篇 report 的結構。

中文思維轉換：從「這個方法是否符合企業情境、證據和限制」開始，再決定英文段落如何下判斷。

Sample Page 1

Assignment brief breakdown.

Brief wordingWhat the learner must do中文提示Evidence to prepare
Explain the current marketing problem.Identify the business issue, target customers and why the current approach is not enough.指出週末客流、學生家庭客群、現有渠道不足。中文審題時要問：到底是哪一類顧客沒有被有效觸達？目前方法為何不夠？Sales pattern, customer observation, competitor comparison, local area notes.
Recommend a suitable promotional approach.Choose realistic marketing actions that fit budget, business size and customer group.建議要可執行，不能像大公司 campaign；要符合小店資源。中文思考時先排除「燒錢廣告」「明星代言」這類不合情境的方案，再選低成本、高可操作性的方案。Social media plan, loyalty card outline, partner bookstore idea, simple cost estimate.
Evaluate likely limits.Show what might reduce success and how the business could monitor results.Distinction 類寫法通常需要看到限制、風險、條件和衡量方式。中文常見錯誤是只寫「這個方法有優點和缺點」，但沒有判斷什麼情況下有效、什麼情況下失效。Budget risk, staff time, weak data, seasonality, tracking method.
中文審題支架

每一句 brief 都要拆成「動詞 + 對象 + 證據」。

  • Explain：說清原因、關係和後果。
  • Recommend：選一個與企業資源相符的方案。
  • Evaluate：作出有條件的判斷。
常見失分點

中文理解正確，但英文輸出失控。

  • 把「推廣」寫成純廣告口號，沒有 business reasoning。
  • 把「證據」理解成自己編一組數字，造成 academic integrity 風險。
  • 把「評估」寫成最後一句 however，前面沒有任何 evidence support。
Sample Page 2

Pass / Merit / Distinction awareness with academic-integrity boundaries.

Pass-level direction

Describe the business problem and explain one reasonable marketing response with basic evidence. The answer should be clear and relevant, but may remain mostly descriptive.

中文：先做到任務完整、概念正確、證據基本相關。學生要能說清楚：企業遇到什麼問題、建議什麼方法、為什麼這個方法與題目有關。

避免：只寫「咖啡店應該多做宣傳」但沒有說明目標顧客和具體渠道。

P

Merit-level direction

Apply evidence more carefully to the cafe. Compare more than one promotional option and explain why one option fits the business better.

中文：不能只背 marketing mix，要把證據真正放回企業情境。學生應比較 social media、loyalty card、bookstore partnership 的適用性。

升級方法：用「對這家小店而言」來限制分析範圍，避免把大企業答案套進小企業題目。

M

Distinction-level direction

Evaluate trade-offs, limits and conditions. A stronger answer explains what could go wrong, what data is missing and how the owner should judge success.

中文：高分來自更成熟的判斷、更清楚的限制和更穩定的證據使用。要寫出方案成功依賴哪些條件，例如 staff time、學生回流率、社媒觸達率或季節因素。

高分信號：能指出「即使方案便宜，也可能因缺乏執行時間或數據追蹤而效果有限」。

Sample Page 3

Evidence planning matrix.

Claim to makeEvidence neededSource noteWriting use
Weekend sales are weaker than weekday sales.Simple weekly sales comparison or owner observation.Use internal business notes if available; do not invent exact numbers. 中文提醒：如果沒有真實數據，只能寫 owner observation / assumed pattern，不可偽造 sales figure。Use to justify why the campaign targets weekend customers. 中文寫法：這條證據用來證明「問題存在」，不是用來證明「方案一定成功」。
Students may respond to loyalty incentives.Local student footfall, competitor loyalty schemes, short customer survey.Keep the evidence modest; avoid unsupported claims about all students. 中文提醒：不要寫「所有學生都喜歡折扣」，應寫「student customers may be more responsive」。Use to explain why loyalty cards may be suitable but limited. 中文寫法：適合性和限制要一起寫，不能只寫 loyalty card 很好。
A bookstore partnership could extend reach.Shared local customer group and low-cost cross-promotion example.Frame as a realistic option, not a guaranteed sales solution. 中文提醒：partnership 是低成本渠道，不是自動增加銷售的魔法。Use in recommendation and evaluation paragraphs. 中文寫法：先說為何匹配顧客群，再說效果取決於雙方合作頻率和宣傳質量。
Mini Writing Frame

Evaluation paragraph skeleton

Point: The loyalty card may be suitable for Harbour Bean Cafe because ...

Evidence: This is supported by ...

Application: In this business, the evidence matters because ...

Limit: However, the recommendation may be limited by ...

Judgement: Therefore, the owner should ...

中文寫作提示

不要直接翻譯中文作文。

  • 用 evidence suggests that，而不是 I think。
  • 用 this may be suitable because，而不是 this is very good。
  • 用 however / therefore / this depends on 連接判斷。
  • 每段只處理一個清楚觀點，不要把所有想法堆在一起。

中文到英文的轉換：「我認為會員卡很好」應變成 The loyalty card may be suitable because it encourages repeat purchases among nearby student customers.

判斷句模板：「這個方案是否有效，取決於……」可以寫成 The effectiveness of this option depends on whether the cafe can track repeat visits and promote the card consistently.

Chinese Support Layer

How the bilingual scaffolding should appear in the book.

1. 概念層：先把題目讀懂

每個 key task 旁邊提供中文審題問題，例如「題目要我證明什麼？」「這個 business context 限制了哪些選項？」

理解

2. 證據層：再決定能寫什麼

提醒學生區分 real evidence、reasonable assumption、invented claim。中文提示要明確指出哪些寫法會導致證據不足或學術不端風險。

證據

3. 表達層：最後轉成英文段落

提供中文意思到英文 academic phrasing 的轉換，而不是逐字翻譯。重點是把「我覺得」轉成 evidence-based judgement。

Vocabulary is taught as academic use, not just translation.

TermAcademic meaning中文理解Useful phrase
Target marketThe group of customers a business aims to reach.目標顧客群，不是所有可能買東西的人。中文常見誤解是把 customer 寫得太寬，導致 recommendation 沒有焦點。The target market for this campaign is likely to be ... / This group matters because ...
