About OTC

About OTC

Overseas Tutorial Centre (OTC) is an internationally oriented education consultancy and pathway support provider with its base in the United Kingdom.

Originally focused on UK progression routes, OTC is now expanding its international summer alliance and pathway network across Australia, the UK, and selected global education destinations.

Our focus is not only short-term programmes, but long-term educational planning: K-12 preparation, private school exposure, pathway readiness, university transition, and family education planning.

Why Australia

Why more families are looking seriously at Australia

Australia is increasingly seen not just as a summer destination, but as a wider education ecosystem with stronger lifestyle confidence, English exposure, K-12 continuity, and clearer long-term family imagination.

  • Safety and lifestyleFamilies often prioritise safety, care structures and quality of life.
  • Boarding and homestayShort-term experiences can more naturally feed into boarding, guardian planning and K-12 decisions.
  • Pathway flexibilityAustralia offers clearer continuity from short-term exposure to university planning.
OTC Gold Coast Focus

Gold Coast is not just a tourism city in OTC's model

OTC treats Gold Coast as an education node where English immersion, school exposure, homestay, lifestyle confidence and future boarding discussion can all sit together.

Current alliance development spans Gold Coast, Brisbane, Sydney, Melbourne, Adelaide and Perth.

Some private school observation opportunities remain subject to school approval.
