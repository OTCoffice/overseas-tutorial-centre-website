Administrative, bilingual and market-entry coordination for overseas education and business organisations preparing to establish a practical presence in Australia.

Practical landing support, with regulated advice kept in the right hands.

OTC supports overseas organisations with the administrative and commercial work around Australian market entry: document organisation, local coordination, supplier communication, education-market research and professional referral. Where Australian immigration, legal, tax, financial or regulated education advice is required, OTC signposts clients to appropriately qualified professionals.

Compliance Boundary

Clear limits protect clients and OTC.

Business Coordination

Administrative planning, commercial communication, records organisation and local service coordination.

Within OTC scope.
Company and Address Support

Practical liaison with ASIC registered agents, corporate service providers, office suppliers and accountants.

Coordination only.
Migration and Visa Matters

Visa strategy, sponsor advice, nomination eligibility and application preparation require an Australian registered migration agent or legal practitioner.

Referral required.
Tax, Legal and Regulated Education Advice

GST, BAS, payroll, contracts, director duties, CRICOS, RTO or Australian education-provider compliance require qualified advisers.

Referral required.
Important professional boundary

OTC provides administrative, market-entry and business coordination support only. OTC does not provide Australian migration, legal, tax, financial or regulated education compliance advice.

Where regulated advice is required, clients should consult registered migration agents, Australian legal practitioners, registered tax/BAS agents, accountants, education compliance specialists or other appropriately qualified professionals.
