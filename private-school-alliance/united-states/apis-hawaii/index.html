Asia Pacific International School, Hawaiʻi

A WASC-accredited private international school for Grades 3–12, with boarding available for Grades 5–12 and rolling admissions.

Application requirements

Online application

SSAT application portal · school code 1401

The application includes two recommendations. Families should also submit report cards or progress reports from the past two years; high-school students submit a transcript.

After initial submission

Tour or virtual interview

After the application and academic records are complete, applicants can arrange a campus tour or virtual interview with the admissions office.

Free OTC application support

OTC can support initial assessment, school matching, application preparation, interview preparation, pre-departure guidance and continuing academic supervision after enrolment.

01Family screening

Grade, academic record, English level, boarding preference and budget.

02Document planning

Application, school reports or transcript, recommendations and interview schedule.

03Submission follow-up

Support with application tracking, supplementary documents and school communication.

Scholarships and costs

Scholarships and financial aid may be available to qualified students. APIS does not offer full scholarships or sports scholarships. Tuition, boarding and all other charges must be confirmed against the school’s latest official fee schedule.

OTC application routeFree application support

No OTC agency fee for the standard school application service. School application charges, translation, notarisation, visa, travel and other third-party costs are excluded and confirmed separately.
