Service Layers

How OTC structures application support.

01

Country

UK, Australia, Canada, US, Europe and Asia-Pacific admission routes.

Different document and timing logic by jurisdiction.
02

Institution

University-level profile, ranking band, admission portal and agent/partner status.

Official entry requirements stay linked to source pages.
03

School

Business School, Media, Engineering, Education, Computing and other academic units.

Each school may have different PS, portfolio or reference rules.
04

Programme

Course-level record with requirements, application status, intake, fee and document checklist.

OTC review can generate a personalised next-step plan.
