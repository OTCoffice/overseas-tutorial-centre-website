Programme & Learning Architecture

Courses

這一頁現在只做一件事：把兩個不同系統分開講清楚。世界高校課程庫放在一邊，OTC 自己的學習平台放在另一邊。

Two Systems

先分清楚：你找的是「全球課程」，還是「OTC 自己的學習支持」？

如果你要找世界 3000 多所高校的 programme、國家、學校、school、入學要求與申請入口，那是 course finder。若你要找 OTC 自己的輔導、題庫、Apps、OTHM、external support 和 progression guidance，那是 learning platform。

System 01 Global Course Finder

世界高校 programme database、國家分流、學校清單、progression route、pathway provider 和申請初篩。

Open course finder System 02 OTC Learning Platform

把國際課程輔導、Apps & Tools、OTHM、external programme support、academic tutoring 和 guidance 全部放在同一個學習平台入口。

Open learning platform
Global Course Finder

世界 3000+ 高校課程，不應放在 OTC 教學入口裡。

這部分應集中放在 University Applications / Programme Directory 體系。它本質上是 course database，不是 OTC 自有課程頁。

Finder 01 Programme Directory

按國家、院校、school/faculty、programme level 做篩選，這裡才是全球課程庫的主入口。

Open directory Finder 02 Country & Institution Routes

先按國家和院校方向分流，再進 programme list，避免把所有課程堆在一個平面頁面上。

Open route Finder 03 Pathway Provider Routes

Study Group、Kaplan、INTO、Navitas、Cambridge Education Group 等 provider-driven 路線也放進課程庫邏輯裡。

Open route Finder 04 Advanced Entry / Top-up Cases

對高意圖的 Year 2、final-year、top-up、mature entry 需求，直接進入特殊路線頁，而不是混在普通課程庫裡。

Open route
OTC Learning Platform

OTC 自己的學習平台，應該單獨成系統。

這部分不是世界高校資料庫，而是 OTC 自有的學習支持、工具、資格路線與教學產品。使用者來這裡，不是為了找校，而是為了學、練、補、提分、做 progression 準備。

Learn 01 International Curriculum Tutoring

A-Level、BTEC、IB、AP、Foundation、pathway learners 的雙語學習支持。

Open learning route Learn 02 Apps & Tools

口語練習、詞彙卡、mock tests、book-linked 工具和可持續增長的學習產品。

Open tools Learn 03 OTHM Qualifications

OTC 受監管資格路線、approved status、level map 和 progression indication。

Open qualification route Learn 04 External Programme Support

OU、Pearson、BTEC、university module、distance learning 和外部註冊學生支持。

Open support route Learn 05 Academic Tutoring

學術寫作、研究方法、商科、管理、英語輸出與學習技能支持。

Open tutoring Learn 06 Guidance & Progression

把學習、top-up、轉學、升學、mature entry 和路線規劃接起來。

How to Open the Right System

很簡單：找校，就去 course finder；要學，就去 learning platform。

這樣一拆，世界高校 programme 不會把 OTC 的學習產品淹沒；OTC 的 Apps、課程輔導和 OTHM 也不會被誤解成「全球課程庫的一部分」。

Use 01 I want to browse universities and programmes

走 Global Course Finder。

Open finder Use 02 I want tutoring, tools or study support

走 OTC Learning Platform。

Open learning Use 03 I already have prior study and need top-up / advanced entry

先走特殊路線，不用先埋在一般課程庫裡。

All OTC course entrances

下面保留 OTC 原有課程與服務入口，方便你按系統類型直接進入對應頁面。

01

International Curriculum Bilingual Tutoring

A-Level, BTEC, IB, AP, IGCSE, OSSD, Australian, US high school, Foundation and pathway learners who need bilingual subject and academic-writing support.

雙語輔導 only. OTC supports understanding, planning and writing skills, not official delivery or assessed-work completion. 02

OTHM Qualifications

Regulated qualification delivery, organised by RQF level and official specification. Delivery only where OTC has current approval, staffing, resources and cohort arrangements.

Approved / current / TBC status must be checked before enrolment. 03

External Programme Support

OU, BTEC, Pearson, university module, foundation / pathway and other recognised external-programme support.

Academic tutoring only. Learners remain registered with their own awarding body, university, college or course provider. 04

Academic Tutoring

Structured support in writing, business, management, research skills, academic English, study skills and progression preparation.

Focused on independent learning, not assignment completion or model answers. 05

Guidance & Progression

Study-route planning, top-up route review, academic profile organisation and ongoing learner guidance packages.

Planning support only; not guaranteed admission, visa, credit-transfer or placement service. 06

University Applications

Country, institution, school and programme-based application screening for UK and international university courses.

透過 OTC 申請. Students can submit a basic profile for eligibility review and next-step advice.
Availability still depends on approval status, staff expertise, learner demand, resources, cohort opening and awarding-body requirements.
