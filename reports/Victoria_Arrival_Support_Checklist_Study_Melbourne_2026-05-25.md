# Victoria Arrival Support Checklist — Study Melbourne Resource Layer

**Date:** 2026-05-25  
**Use:** OTC Australia office internal student-support workflow and 留學導報 article source note.  
**Boundary:** Public student-support orientation only. Not migration, legal, employment, accommodation, medical or regulated advice.

---

## 1. Purpose

This checklist helps OTC organise a compliant Victoria / Melbourne arrival-support layer for international students and families. It uses Study Melbourne and Victorian Government public resources as orientation points, while keeping OTC's role limited to education coordination, document organisation, student communication and public-resource guidance.

中文定位：

本清單用於 OTC 整理赴墨爾本 / 維州學生的落地支持資料。它不是簽證、移民、就業、住宿、醫療或法律建議，而是幫學生在出發前知道：哪些官方資源可以查，哪些事項要提前準備，遇到問題時應如何找到合適渠道。

## 2. Before Departure

| Area | Student action | OTC support boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Offer and enrolment | Confirm offer, CoE, payment record, orientation date and provider contact | Help organise education documents and timeline |
| Accommodation | Review contract, address, arrival date, bond / deposit record and emergency contact | Provide checklist only; no legal advice |
| OSHC and health | Confirm OSHC provider, policy dates and emergency numbers | Help student locate policy documents |
| Banking and money | Prepare card access, emergency funds and bank-opening plan | General planning only |
| Phone and internet | Prepare roaming / SIM plan for first week | General planning only |
| Transport | Check airport transfer, Myki / local transport options and campus route | General orientation |
| Emergency contacts | Save school, accommodation, family, OSHC, emergency and OTC contact details | Provide contact sheet template |

## 3. First Week in Melbourne / Victoria

- Visit or save the Study Melbourne website and Hub details.
- Attend provider orientation and confirm student ID / LMS / email access.
- Check accommodation condition and keep dated photos.
- Set up local phone, banking, transport and health contacts.
- Confirm part-time work rights only through official student visa and Fair Work resources.
- Learn emergency numbers and campus safety contacts.
- Keep copies of passport, visa grant, CoE, OSHC, lease / accommodation record and emergency contacts.

---

## 4. Study Melbourne Resource Layer

Study Melbourne can be introduced to students as a public Victorian Government information and support resource for international students in Victoria.

Useful public-resource categories:

- Study Melbourne Hub and student events.
- Living in Melbourne / Victoria.
- Working while studying and work-rights information.
- Accommodation and cost-of-living information.
- Safety, wellbeing and support links.
- Newsletter and social-media updates.

Safe wording:

> Study Melbourne is a Victorian Government public information and support resource for international students in Victoria. OTC may direct students and families to Study Melbourne resources as part of general orientation. This does not imply partnership, appointment or endorsement.

---

## 5. When to Escalate Beyond OTC

OTC should refer students to appropriate qualified or official channels when the issue involves:

- visa refusal, cancellation, bridging status or migration strategy;
- employment exploitation, unpaid wages or workplace injury;
- accommodation dispute, eviction, bond dispute or contract conflict;
- mental-health crisis, medical emergency or safety risk;
- professional registration or skills assessment;
- legal dispute or police matter.

OTC role: help the student identify the category of issue, preserve documents, contact the education provider support service where appropriate and refer to official / qualified support.
