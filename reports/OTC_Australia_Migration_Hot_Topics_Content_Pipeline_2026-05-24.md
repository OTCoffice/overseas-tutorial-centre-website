## 1. Editorial Position

澳洲移民內容的流量很高，但合規風險也高。OTC / 留學導報應採用「政策解讀 + 文件準備 + 升學職業路徑教育」定位，而不是「保證移民」「代辦簽證」「內部名額」定位。

Recommended public wording:

> 本文為公開政策與教育資訊整理，不構成移民法律建議。簽證、技能評估、職業註冊及個案申請結果，應以澳洲官方機構、註冊移民代理或合資格法律顧問的意見為準。

## 3. First Four Articles to Produce

### Article 1

**Title:** 澳洲 2026 學生簽證不是簡單「擴招」：NPL 295,000 與 MD115 優先處理怎麼看  
**Angle:** Explain that NPL is a managed-growth / processing-prioritisation system rather than a simple public promise that every applicant has a place.  
**Lead points:**

- 2026 NPL is 295,000, 25,000 higher than 2025.
- MD115 replaced MD111 for 2026 offshore student visa processing.
- The NPL system applies to offshore Student visa applications.
- Complete files matter because missing or incorrect documents delay processing and may lead to refusal.

**CTA:** Ask OTC to review course-provider fit, intake timing and document completeness before lodgement.

### Article 2

**Title:** 澳洲 2026-27 永居名額 185,000：為什麼「人在澳洲境內」變得更重要  
**Angle:** Turn the headline number into a planning question: should a student or graduate focus on realistic onshore study/work transition rather than chasing an offshore shortcut?  
**Lead points:**

- 2026-27 permanent Migration Program is set at 185,000 places.
- Approximate 70:30 split between Skilled and Family programs.
- The program composition prioritises onshore migrants already living in Australia.
- This supports OTC's content line: education pathway planning must start early.

**CTA:** Book an education pathway file review, not a visa guarantee.

### Article 3

**Title:** 482 Skills in Demand 簽證：僱主擔保不是「找到公司就行」  
**Angle:** Correct the common misconception that sponsorship is only about having a friendly employer.  
**Lead points:**

- The applicant must be nominated for a skilled position by an approved sponsor.
- Core Skills stream requires an occupation on the Core Skills Occupation List.
- Relevant work experience and English requirements matter.
- Salary must meet the correct threshold and annual market salary rate.

**CTA:** Employer/applicant file-readiness checklist.

### Article 4

**Title:** 澳洲僱主擔保薪資門檻：CSIT、SSIT、AMSR 到底差在哪  
**Angle:** A practical terminology article designed to capture search traffic from people comparing 482/186/494 routes.  
**Lead points:**

- Explain TSMIT, CSIT, SSIT and AMSR in plain Chinese.
- Clarify that non-cash benefits such as accommodation are not a substitute for the required threshold.
- Emphasise that salary thresholds and market salary evidence are compliance issues for employers.

**CTA:** Sponsor-position salary and document audit.
