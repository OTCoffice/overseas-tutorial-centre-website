# OTC Australia Payment Operations Control Note

**Date created:** 2026-05-25  
**Linked evidence:** `reports/OTC_Australia_Airwallex_Account_Verification_Evidence_2026-05-25.md`  
**Purpose:** Internal control note for Australia-facing receipts, payments, reimbursements, provider fees and future commission reconciliation.

---

## 1. Control Principle

OTC should treat the Airwallex verification as an operations-readiness milestone, not as permission to mix all Australia-related funds together. Every Australia-related receipt or outgoing payment should be traceable to:

- a named project or student file;
- an invoice, receipt, email, provider document or platform statement;
- a clear payment type;
- a compliance category.

---

## 2. Payment Categories

Use the following categories in the reconciliation file:

| Category | Use |
| --- | --- |
| OTC service fee | Fees paid to OTC for education advisory, document organisation, training or support services. |
| Provider application fee | Fees paid to an institution, school, pathway provider or application portal. |
| Third-party reimbursement | Costs paid by OTC on behalf of a student / client and later reimbursed. |
| Supplier / platform expense | Business tools, subscriptions, design, translation, document production, office support. |
| Commission / referral income | Only when supported by a signed provider agreement or written commission confirmation. |
| Refund / adjustment | Any repayment, correction or partial reversal. |
| Internal setup cost | Office establishment, compliance preparation, application preparation and business development. |

---

## 3. Minimum Evidence Required

Each payment line should have at least one supporting item:

- invoice number;
- receipt PDF;
- Gmail message ID;
- provider payment instruction;
- Airwallex transaction record;
- internal approval note.

Sensitive account numbers, card numbers, private IDs and student identity documents should be stored in a private finance folder, not in public website or marketing files.

---

## 4. Public Claims Boundary

Safe public wording:

> OTC maintains verified business payment infrastructure for education operations, supplier payments and international business administration.

Do not state or imply:

- Airwallex verification is a provider appointment;
- OTC is authorised to collect tuition on behalf of a provider unless a written agreement confirms this;
- payment infrastructure equals migration-advice authority;
- payment infrastructure equals government endorsement.

---

## 5. Working File

Use:

`reports/OTC_Australia_Payment_Reconciliation_Template_2026-05-25.csv`

Recommended workflow:

1. Duplicate the CSV for each month.
2. Keep one row per transaction.
3. Attach or reference evidence file names.
4. Review monthly before any provider application update.
5. Keep public marketing copy separate from finance records.
