**Status:** Submitted. Not public partnership evidence.

## 1. Official Process Confirmed

Northern Territory Government Department of Education and Training replied to OTC and directed OTC to follow the process on the NT.GOV.AU page:

`https://nt.gov.au/learning/international-education/become-an-education-agent`

The official process states that a company can apply to become an international education agent for the Northern Territory. The application process is:

1. Fill in the International education agent application.
2. Email the application to `internationalservice@education.nt.gov.au`.
3. Wait for written assessment outcome.
4. If approved, receive a formal agent agreement or use the institution's standard agent agreement.
5. Appointment as representative occurs only when both parties agree on terms and the signed agreement is returned.

## 3. Compliance Boundary

Until a formal agent agreement is issued, agreed and returned, OTC must not say or imply:

- OTC is an appointed representative of NT Government Schools.
- OTC is an official NT Government Schools agent.
- OTC is authorised to recruit students for NT Government Schools.
- NT Government has endorsed OTC.

Safe internal wording:

> OTC has received written direction from Northern Territory Government Department of Education and Training to follow the official NT.GOV.AU education agent application process. OTC is preparing the application and supporting documents.

Safe public wording, if needed:

> OTC is reviewing the official Northern Territory Government Schools education agent application process as part of its Australia school-route development work.
