Date: 2026-05-29

Method: immediate public-web supplement using official provider pages or official brochures/search snippets. Use as candidate research only. Do not describe any provider as an OTC official partner until an agreement is signed.

## Main Finding

Ireland is strong for junior English + residential / homestay routes. Singapore is stronger for day-based English, STEM, leadership and short academic enrichment; overseas accommodation / guardian logistics will need extra handling for many Singapore options.

## Top 5 Ireland Candidates

1. Irish College of English (ICE) - published 2026 dates, boarding-school campus and homestay options; strongest immediate Ireland lead.
2. ATC Language Schools - mainstream Dublin English route with multiple campus and accommodation choices.
3. UCD Summer High School Program - university-official route for high-school subject and undergraduate planning.
4. Emerald Cultural Institute - useful for English plus Cambridge FCE / CAE preparation positioning.
5. ISI Dublin - city-centre Dublin candidate with residential 14-17 and parent-facing language support.

## Top 5 Singapore Candidates

1. NUS SCALE Youth Programmes - top university official route, minimum 15, online/on-campus flexibility and 2026 open-enrolment signal.
2. SMU Young Leaders - strong university-official leadership route, but 2026 is closed and should be treated as 2027 pipeline.
3. Immerse Education Singapore - residential academic-enrichment brand with international student mix.
4. British Council Singapore - trusted low-age English day-camp anchor for 4-16.
5. Embassy Camps Singapore - differentiated skills-plus-English candidate; needs provider and logistics verification.
