# OTC Ireland / Singapore Summer Alliance Web Research

Date: 2026-05-29

Method: immediate public-web supplement using official provider pages or official brochures/search snippets. Use as candidate research only. Do not describe any provider as an OTC official partner until an agreement is signed.

## Main Finding

Ireland is strong for junior English + residential / homestay routes. Singapore is stronger for day-based English, STEM, leadership and short academic enrichment; overseas accommodation / guardian logistics will need extra handling for many Singapore options.

## Top 5 Ireland Candidates

1. DCU International Academy - strongest group residential campus route.
2. ISI Dublin - clearest pricing and dates for individual residential juniors.
3. Babel Academy - good residence/homestay mix for 11-17.
4. Swan at UCD - UCD campus route with clear 12-17 positioning.
5. Welcome Ireland - useful lower-age 9-17 Dublin route with residence/homestay choice.

## Top 5 Singapore Candidates

1. British Council Singapore - safest English-learning day-camp anchor.
2. SMU Young Leaders - strongest high-school leadership / pre-university route.
3. NUS College Summer School - premium 16-18 university-exposure candidate.
4. HWA x SUSS-INTO - broad 5-19 international-school / future-skills option.
5. MDIS English Immersion - strongest group English-immersion route if OTC can form 30+ students.

## Missing Questions For Outreach

- Do you accept individual overseas junior students, or groups only?
- Is accommodation included? If not, can you support guardian / homestay / hotel arrangements?
- What are the exact 2026 fees, arrival dates, minimum stay and refund terms?
- What age ranges are accepted for each course?
- Are airport transfers, meals, insurance and activities included?
- Do you work with overseas education consultants / referral partners?
- Are there commission, net/gross pricing or tagged application-link arrangements?
- What documents are required for non-EU / non-Singapore students?
- What welfare / safeguarding ratio applies to minors?
