## Product Position

The BTEC Level 3 Business Assignment Writing Toolkit is the first executable product in the OTC bilingual study-support publishing route for Chinese international-school, international-department and pathway learners.

It is not a textbook replacement and not an official Pearson / BTEC publication. The product trains learners to read assignment briefs, understand criteria, plan evidence, write clear English academic paragraphs and avoid academic misconduct.

## Target Readers

- China-based BTEC Level 3 / International Level 3 Business learners
- Pearson Vocational learners preparing for undergraduate progression
- International-school and international-department students with EAL needs
- Pathway learners who understand business concepts but struggle with English assignment output
- Tutors who need a bilingual planning and feedback resource

## Core Pain Points

1. Learners read the brief but miss task scope, command verbs and evidence requirements.
2. Learners confuse Pass / Merit / Distinction criteria with generic effort levels.
3. Learners collect sources late and cannot link evidence to business arguments.
4. Learners over-translate from Chinese and write descriptive paragraphs.
5. Learners use AI or online material without understanding academic-integrity boundaries.
