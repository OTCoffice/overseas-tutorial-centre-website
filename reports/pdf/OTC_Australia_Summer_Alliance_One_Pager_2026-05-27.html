What OTC is building
OTC is extending from UK-facing pathway and admissions support into an Australia Summer Alliance model. The offer is not a generic tour. It combines academic English, cultural immersion, private-school experience, future boarding-pathway positioning and screened family communication.
Why Gold Coast
Gold Coast is unusually strong for this model: strong parent acceptance, safer lifestyle perception, lower cost than UK/US, enough private-school density, and better packaging potential for homestay + English + school exposure.

Important boundary
OTC does not publicly promise guaranteed boarding, immersion, school placement, commission terms or institutional appointment. Private-school experience and short-term exposure routes are always presented as subject to school approval and case-by-case confirmation.
