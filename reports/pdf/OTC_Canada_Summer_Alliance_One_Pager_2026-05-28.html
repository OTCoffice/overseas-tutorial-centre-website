What OTC is building
OTC is extending its Summer Alliance screening from the UK, Australia and New Zealand into Canada, with a clearer distinction between city-based homestay routes, residence-based university-campus routes, English improvement and future-facing academic or leadership demand.
Why Canada
For many families, Canada is attractive because it combines North American campus exposure with stronger safety perception, structured supervision, practical English immersion and a more balanced family-facing tone than some higher-pressure summer markets.

What direct cooperation would improve
Cleaner family-facing positioning · better separation between homestay and residence expectations · more accurate custodial / consent explanation · better pre-screening before enquiries reach provider admissions and registration teams · more credible progression from secondary-agent handling toward direct partnership.
Current cooperation boundary
OTC currently approaches Canada routes conservatively. Some enquiries may begin via secondary-agent or open registration channels, while direct cooperation is discussed only where provider response, family fit and route clarity make it appropriate. No public promise is made regarding exclusive representation, guaranteed commission terms or direct appointment without written confirmation.
Next step
OTC seeks a practical first discussion on referral flow, suitable student profile, custodial / welfare handling, family screening quality and whether selected Canada summer routes may support a more direct cooperation model.
