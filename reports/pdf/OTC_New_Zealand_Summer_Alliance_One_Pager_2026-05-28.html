What OTC is building
OTC is extending from UK-facing admissions and pathway support into a New Zealand Summer Alliance model. The offer is not a generic tour catalogue. It is built around screened family communication, clearer route separation, and more practical distinctions between junior English, holiday programme, school-preparation and university-transition demand.
Why New Zealand
For many families, New Zealand is attractive because it offers a calmer first international route, stronger homestay confidence, school-facing environments and a better fit for English-plus-preparation decisions rather than purely commercial camp positioning.

What direct cooperation would improve
Clearer family-facing communication · more accurate age / welfare / homestay positioning · better pre-screening before enquiries reach provider teams · cleaner route separation between holiday, school-prep, group and university-transition demand.
Important boundary
OTC does not publicly promise guaranteed placement, direct-agent status, school admission, commission terms or provider appointment without written confirmation. Current New Zealand routes may include information support, secondary-agent channels or direct cooperation discussions depending on provider response and current arrangement.
Next step
