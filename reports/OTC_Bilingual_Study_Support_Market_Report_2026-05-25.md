# OTC Bilingual Study Support Market Report

Date: 2026-05-25
Owner: Overseas Publishing House / OTC Study Hub
Status: Working market report and publishing roadmap

## Executive Position

OTC should treat Chinese international schools, international departments, bilingual schools and international pathway centres as a core bilingual publishing market. The opportunity is not to replace official materials from Pearson, Cambridge, OxfordAQA, IB or College Board. The opportunity is to publish independent bilingual study-support resources that help Chinese EAL learners move from subject understanding to responsible English academic output.

The strongest near-term publishing route is BTEC Level 3 / International Level 3, because it connects vocational progression, university-entry planning, assignment-based learning and Chinese learners' need for bilingual scaffolding. BTEC Higher Nationals Level 4 / 5 HNC-HND should remain the longer-term centre-delivery and advanced study-support route.

## Market Logic

China's international education market has shifted from rapid school growth into quality competition. Private bilingual schools, public-school international departments, pathway centres and vocational international programmes all need better learning support for students who are studying in English but thinking, planning and revising partly in Chinese.

Official curriculum systems remain strong:

- Cambridge International IGCSE and A Level remain major global school qualifications.
- Pearson Edexcel International GCSE and International A Level sit inside Pearson's international school pathway.
- Pearson BTEC International Level 3 is designed to support progression to higher education, Higher Nationals or employment.
- IB programmes continue to occupy the high-end bilingual and international-school segment.

The market gap is not qualification availability. The gap is bilingual learning infrastructure: glossary, assignment interpretation, command-word coaching, evidence planning, paragraph frames, rubric reading and academic-integrity boundaries.

## Why BTEC Level 3 Matters

BTEC Level 3 should not be treated as secondary to Higher Nationals. For OTC, Level 3 is the immediate publishing and bilingual-study-support market.

Strategic value:

- It serves Chinese international-school and pathway learners before undergraduate entry.
- It is assignment-rich, making bilingual planning resources genuinely useful.
- It can produce repeatable products in Business, IT, Creative Media, Hospitality, Health & Social Care and Applied Science.
- It supports OTC's public positioning around university progression, not only vocational delivery.
- It creates a content and audience bridge toward BTEC Higher Nationals.

## ISBN Allocation Policy

ISBNs should be assigned only to publication-ready products, not to ideas, drafts or internal previews.

Rules:

- One formal sales book = one ISBN.
- PDF ebook, paperback/POD and EPUB/Kindle editions should be treated as separate formats.
- Minor typo fixes do not require a new ISBN.
- New edition, substantial content change, title change, separate Chinese/English/bilingual versions, or teacher/student editions should receive separate ISBNs.
- Apps and browser tools usually do not require ISBNs unless packaged with a formal ebook or PDF publication.
