Institutional Cooperation

University Agent & Institutional Cooperation

For university admissions teams, international offices and partner managers reviewing OTC's application-support capability.

OTC Position

UK-based education coordination for Chinese and international students.

Overseas Tutorial Centre Ltd supports students and families with education-route planning, university application preparation, bilingual document organisation, transcript review, course mapping, English-readiness planning and admissions communication. OTC is interested in formal university representative arrangements where the institution's policies, training, data rules and admissions processes can be followed properly.

Unless a written institutional agreement says otherwise, references to universities on this website are contextual application information and do not imply endorsement, official partnership or guaranteed admission.

Recruitment Support

Student enquiry handling, course screening, document checklist preparation, admissions question drafting and offer-condition follow-up.

02

China Programme Cases

Repeated demand from students in Chinese university international programmes who need UK Year 2, top-up, direct-entry or alternative-route advice.

03

Document Discipline

Structured student files, version control, transcript translation checks, module mapping and clear distinction between source evidence and adviser notes.

04

Compliance Boundary

No false documents, no guarantee claims, no immigration advice unless referred to a qualified adviser, and no implication of official university relationship without written permission.

05

Operational Follow-up

OTC can maintain enquiry logs, deadline reminders, missing-document lists, student/parent communication records and admissions-team follow-up notes.

06

Partner Readiness

Where approved by a university, OTC can follow agent training, portal requirements, branding rules, data-protection obligations and reporting expectations.

Cooperation Request

What OTC would like to discuss with universities

OTC would like to understand the university's official representative process, agent onboarding requirements, territory or student-category rules, training expectations, application portal process, data-protection requirements and whether advanced-entry pre-checks can be handled through a defined channel.
