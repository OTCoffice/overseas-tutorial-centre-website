Which route might be relevant?

The Global Talent route is for people aged 18 or over who are leaders or potential leaders in academia or research, arts and culture, or digital technology. Most applicants need an endorsement before applying for the visa. Holders of an eligible prestigious prize may apply without an endorsement.

Each field has its own endorsing body and evidence requirements. We map public guidance against existing achievements and flag case-specific questions for a regulated professional; an endorsing body alone decides endorsement.

What OTC can deliver

An agreed package may include a route and official-source checklist, an index of achievements and public links, a gaps and questions log, a document version index, and a preparation timeline. We help organise the factual story of your work and improve clarity and consistency.

References must be verified and signed by genuine referees. We do not invent awards, roles, income, press coverage or relationships, and cannot decide whether evidence meets an endorsing body’s criteria.

How the preparation works

1. Share your field, residence and public work → 2. Review official routes and agree scope → 3. Confirm fees and deliverables in writing → 4. Build the evidence index → 5. Follow up and check versions → 6. You and, where needed, a regulated adviser verify the formal application.

Endorsement and visa application are separate stages. Endorsement alone does not grant a visa. You retain control of accounts, passwords, verification codes, payments and final submission, or arrange formal representation with a qualified professional.

Fees, timing and professional boundaries

The initial enquiry is free. Preparation support is quoted by field, volume of material, follow-up period and revision rounds. We agree deliverables, timing, payment, cancellation and refund terms in writing before work starts. Government charges, the immigration health surcharge, translation, certification and external professional fees are separate unless expressly included.

As checked on 27 September 2026, GOV.UK lists a £766 main applicant fee, split into £561 for endorsement and £205 for the visa on the endorsement route. The health surcharge is usually additional. Recheck official rates before payment. OTC does not guarantee endorsement, a visa, processing time or settlement. For eligibility decisions, complex immigration history, refusals, appeals or representation, consult an IAA-registered adviser or an authorised legal professional.

Common questions

Do I need a UK job offer? The digital technology Global Talent route does not require one. Check the specific rules for other fields; this route differs from Skilled Worker sponsorship.

Is settlement automatic? No. The earliest point to apply may be 3 or 5 years depending on the route, and the applicant must meet the applicable residence, earnings and other requirements.

Can I start with a rough CV? Yes. Share a brief summary and public links first. No passport, bank records, original references or login details are needed for an initial enquiry.
