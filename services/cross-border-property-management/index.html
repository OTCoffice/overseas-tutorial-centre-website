Service Overview

What this service does

A bilingual owner-side coordination service for overseas property. OTC organises the working file, communication trail, supplier quotes, maintenance log and regular owner report so that decisions can be made without fragmented cross-border messages.

面向持有海外房產、需要雙語行政協調、供應商對接、租務資訊整理與定期業主報告的個人及家庭。

Workflow

Process

  1. Property and authority intake
  2. Local contact and responsibility map
  3. Document and condition-file setup
  4. Supplier, occupier and agent coordination
  5. Issue tracking and owner approval
  6. Monthly, quarterly or case-close reporting

Deliverables

Fees

Indicative pricing
