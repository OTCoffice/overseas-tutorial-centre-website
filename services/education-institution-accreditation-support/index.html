Service Overview

What this service does

A documentation and readiness service for education providers preparing approval, accreditation or quality-assurance evidence packs.

面向準備機構申請、審核或資質整理的培訓機構、補習中心、學院與教育公司。

Workflow

Process

  1. Readiness diagnostic
  2. Policy and evidence map
  3. Document gap list
  4. Evidence pack preparation
  5. Quality narrative and submission support
