Reviewing an existing draft

Review the central argument, evidence selection, paragraph flow and course fit, with comments and revision priorities.

Improving English expression

Preparing course-specific versions

Check each prompt, word limit and course focus before adapting your statement.

Applications covered

Hong Kong Associate Degree / Higher Diploma progression, overseas undergraduate and master’s applications. Personal statements, SOPs and short-answer questions are reviewed against the actual programme requirements.

For an existing English draft for a UK master’s application, see our UK SOP editing service and indicative fees (Chinese). Those fees do not automatically apply to other application types or support without a draft.

Turn experience into a clear explanation

Hypothetical teaching example: “I completed a group project, so I am interested in this subject.”

We help you identify your role, methods, challenges, learning and connection to the target course. We do not add invented achievements or figures.

Keep your own voice and check the institution’s rules on outside help and AI use. You review and confirm the final content.

Process and deliverables

  1. Share programme links, deadline and time zone, and draft status.
  2. Agree scope, total fee, revision rounds and delivery dates in writing.
  3. Organise genuine evidence and prepare your draft.
  4. Receive comments, supply missing information and revise; reviews follow the agreed rounds.
  5. Check facts, prompt, word count and version before your final approval and submission.

Deliverables may include a material bank, outline, annotated draft, tracked changes and final checklist. Your agreed scope specifies what is included.

Scope and fees

Standalone support: quoted by draft condition, length, programme versions, revision rounds and deadline. Hong Kong application support carries a service fee agreed after reviewing your needs.

Application packages: for eligible partner applications covered by free agency support, confirm which standard document assistance is included. Standalone detailed editing, translation and extra versions are not automatically free.

University application fees, tuition, examinations, visa and other third-party costs are separate. Total fees, applicable tax, timing, payment, cancellation and refund arrangements are agreed before work starts.

Frequently asked questions

No draft, or only Chinese notes?

Ask about planning and material organisation; these are quoted separately from editing an existing English draft.

Can I request one statement only?

Yes. A full application package is not required.

How long does it take?

We confirm timing based on the materials, deadline and availability. Urgent delivery must be agreed first.

Do you guarantee an offer?

No. Admissions decisions remain with the institution.

What should I send first?

Programme links, deadline and draft status are enough to start. No identity documents are needed; drafts may have names, student numbers and contact details removed.
