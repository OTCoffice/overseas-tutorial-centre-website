How we can help

  • No draft yet: discuss your experience, choose key points and prepare an outline to help you start writing.
  • Existing draft: improve the structure and flag gaps in the content.
  • English editing: improve wording and sentences while keeping your meaning.
  • Different programmes: adapt each version to its prompt, word limit and course requirements.

For a class project, for example, explain your role, what you learned and how it connects to your choice of subject.

Getting started

Send programme links, your deadline and whether you have a draft. We will agree the work, fee, revision rounds and delivery dates.

Depending on the agreed scope, you receive an outline, comments or tracked edits. You add missing information and revise, then we review. You approve the final content and submit it.

You can ask for help with one statement, Chinese notes or planning from scratch. Urgent work depends on availability.

Fees

Fees depend on length, draft condition, programme versions and revision rounds. Hong Kong application support and standalone statement services are chargeable.

For overseas applications eligible for free agency support, we explain which document assistance is included. Extra editing or translation, university application fees, tuition and other third-party costs are separate.

Total fees, applicable tax, delivery, payment, cancellation and refund arrangements are confirmed in writing before work starts.

For an existing UK master’s draft in English, see UK SOP editing and indicative fees (Chinese). Those fees apply only to the stated scope.

Before you submit

Your statement must reflect your own experience and follow the institution’s rules on outside help and AI use. We do not invent experience or guarantee admission.

No identity documents are needed for an initial enquiry. You may remove your name, student number and contact details from drafts.
