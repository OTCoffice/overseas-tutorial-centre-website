READ THE PASSAGEThe town library began opening an hour earlier. At first, few people arrived before nine, but within a month students had begun using the quiet space to study before school.
QUESTION 04 · DETAIL根據文章作答

What changed over the month?

AThe library closed
BStudents increasingly visited early
CSchool started later
DThe library moved
04

答案與解析

Answer Key & Explanations · 點擊題號展開

答案速查　01 B　·　02 B　·　03 C　·　04 B

01 · B — clear

Lucid 意為「清晰、容易理解的」，最接近 clear。Obscure 表示模糊不清，與題意相反。

02 · B — bird

種子可以生長為樹，蛋可以孵化為鳥。類比題要先判斷兩個詞之間的關係，再尋找相同的關係。

03 · C — $60

折扣為 $80 × 25% = $20；售價為 $80 − $20 = $60。也可直接計算 $80 × 75% = $60。

04 · B — Students increasingly visited early

文章先說早上九點前人很少，後來學生開始在上學前使用圖書館，因此 B 最符合文章。其餘選項均未在文章中提及。
