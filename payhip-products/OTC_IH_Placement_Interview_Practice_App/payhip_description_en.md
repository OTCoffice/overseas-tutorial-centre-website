# OTC IH Placement & Interview Practice App

An independent OTC downloadable practice app for learners preparing for IH-style pre-arrival online placement tests and speaking interviews.

The app runs locally in a browser. No installation is required. After download, open `README_START_HERE.html` or `app/index.html`.

## Included

- A1-B2 levelled placement quiz
- Sequential unlock logic: A2 opens only after A1 completion and accuracy threshold
- Bilingual feedback after wrong answers
- Levelled vocabulary recognition test
- English-meaning matching first, with Chinese support after answering
- Speaking interview practice by CEFR level
- Multiple interview topics per level
- Follow-up question practice
- Mock interview timer
- Interview survival phrases
- Teacher mode
- Speaking rubric
- Weak words and weak prompts tracking
- Teacher handover / parent update summary generator

## Suitable for

- Learners preparing for IH London or similar English summer-course placement
- Students who need to practise online placement-test style questions
- Students preparing for a short speaking interview or teacher conversation
- Parents who want a structured pre-arrival diagnostic tool
- Tutors who need a ready-to-use placement and interview practice app

## Disclaimer

This is an independent OTC practice resource. It is not an official International House London publication, official placement test, official interview script or guaranteed-placement product. Learners should follow direct instructions from their school and complete the official test using the link supplied by that school.
