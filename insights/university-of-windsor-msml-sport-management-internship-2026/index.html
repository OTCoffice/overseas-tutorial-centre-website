A fact-checked guide to the University of Windsor's internship-based Master of Sport Management & Leadership: 16–20 months, Fall entry only, a 360-hour placement, academic documents and English-language requirements.

Programme overview

The University of Windsor's internship-based Master of Sport Management & Leadership combines graduate study with a 360-hour placement in the sport and recreation sector. The usual duration is 16–20 months, with Fall entry only and a published application deadline of February 1.

The curriculum includes Research Methods, Social Issues in Sport Management, the internship, Critical Reflections and Industry Insights, and MSML electives covering leadership, organisational behaviour, marketing, strategy, media and communication.

Academic and application requirements

Applicants need a four-year bachelor's degree in Sport Management and Leadership or a related field, with a cumulative average of at least 70% over the final two years of full-time-equivalent study. International grades are assessed through the university's own equivalency process.

The internship stream also requires two academic references, the MSML Internship Supplementary Admissions Form and a résumé. Prior training, experience and accomplishments matter, so applicants should document sport clubs, events, teams, community sport, marketing, media and leadership work with specific evidence.

English-language requirements

The published minimums include IELTS Academic 7.0, TOEFL iBT 100, Duolingo 130, Pearson PTE 68, CAEL 70 or Cambridge English Advanced/Proficiency 185.

The university offers English-language programmes, but applicants should not assume automatic conditional admission to MSML. Any language-pathway arrangement should be confirmed in writing by Graduate Studies and the programme.

Application strategy

A strong application connects prior experience to a clear sport-industry problem and explains how the MSML curriculum and internship support the next career step. References should give concrete evidence of academic ability, reliability, collaboration and leadership potential.

Because the programme has Fall entry only and experiential spaces are limited, applicants should prepare language scores, references, the résumé and supplementary form well before the deadline. Meeting the published minimum does not guarantee admission.
