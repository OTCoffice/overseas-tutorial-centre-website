Career Contract Signing Checklist:
Company Checks, Clauses and Negotiation

職業簽約前怎樣看？公司核查、七類條款與談判清單

A practical checklist for checking a contracting company, mapping obligations and preparing negotiation points before signing a career agreement.

A practical checklist for checking a contracting company, mapping obligations and preparing negotiation points before signing a career agreement.

Confirm who you are contracting with

Check the legal name, registration number, jurisdiction, signing party and payment recipient. A brand name or social profile may not be the contracting entity.

Public records are useful for risk screening but do not establish that a contract is safe or enforceable.

Review seven clause groups

Map both sides’ obligations, termination and renewal, revenue and deductions, rights permissions, exclusivity and non-compete limits, upfront charges, and every referenced schedule.

Where your duties are precise but the company’s commitments are vague, ask for measurable deliverables, dates and remedies in writing.

Turn concerns into negotiation points

Classify each point as must change, must clarify or acceptable. Record the original wording, practical risk, requested revision and fallback position, then compare every later version.

Before signing, confirm page count, schedules, dates, signatures and that both parties retain the same final version.

Know when a lawyer is required

Seek qualified legal advice for substantial penalties, long exclusivity, cross-border disputes, broad restraints, perpetual rights, personal guarantees or an existing dispute.

Process support can organise facts, files and negotiation tasks, but it is not legal advice and cannot guarantee amendments or outcomes.
