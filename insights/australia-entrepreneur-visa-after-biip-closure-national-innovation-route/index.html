What Entrepreneurs Should Understand About the National Innovation Visa

Australia’s BIIP subclass 188 program is closed to new applications. This briefing explains why ordinary small-business migration narratives need to change and how the National Innovation Visa priorities frame promising entrepreneurial activities.

The old business migration story no longer works

Australia’s Business Innovation and Investment Program is closed to new applications. Entrepreneurs should not rely on outdated subclass 188 narratives when planning a new Australia route.

The National Innovation Visa is not a replacement for every small business owner. It is aimed at exceptional talent and innovation potential.
