What IH is checking

Level fit, not perfection

The purpose is to place the learner in an age- and level-appropriate class, so answers should be natural, clear and honest.

Route facts

Typical IH preparation route

Students usually complete an online level placement test before the course starts so the school can place them in a suitable class.

The speaking interview or teacher conversation is used to check spoken confidence, interaction and level fit.

7-day plan

Short, steady preparation

  1. Diagnostic grammar + 2 speaking themes.
  2. School, hobbies and family answers.
  3. London travel and summer course vocabulary.
  4. Past experience and future plan grammar.
  5. Opinions, reasons and examples.
  6. Full mock interview with timer.
  7. Weak-word review and calm final rehearsal.
